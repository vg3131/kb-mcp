# Aqua Zero Floor Pads

Aqua Zeros are wet-process surface polish tools (50–400 grit) designed to clean and prepare a burnished or trowelled concrete surface for a nil-exposure finish. "Aqua" = used wet, "Zero" = minimal stock removal. Most critical factor: selecting the correct starting grit based on the existing surface texture.

## Tag

Surface Polish · Wet

## Steps

### Step 1: Assess surface texture

This is the most important step. If the finish is high-quality power-trowelled concrete, start at 400 Aqua Zero. If the finish is borderline or rougher, start at 200 Aqua Zero to protect the 400 from wearing prematurely. Do not skip this assessment.

### Step 2: Set water delivery

Water must go directly into the back of the machine — NOT pooling in front. Good skirt seal = water pressure maintained under the machine, keeping tools cool and flushing slurry. Target 6–10 litres per minute.

### Step 3: Clean the surface first

Remove all dust, debris and grit before running Aqua Zeros. Any particles under the machine will scratch the surface — on a surface polish there is nowhere to hide mistakes.

### Step 4: Run 200 Aqua Zero (if required)

Removes surface texture, making the 400 more effective and extending its life. Inspect the surface after each pass. The goal is a consistent, slightly opened texture across the whole floor.

### Step 5: Run 400 Aqua Zero

Refines the surface. Monitor tool wear regularly — if wearing too fast, increase water or check surface prep. Consistent speed and overlapping passes are essential.

### Step 6: Clean and apply Lithium Boost

Thoroughly clean the floor after Aqua Zero work. Apply Totally Boost Lithium via microfibre. Allow to dry fully.

### Step 7: Transition to resins

If you started at 400 Aqua Zero, begin resins at 100 or 200 Universal. If you started at 200 AZ, begin at 100 Universal. Finish to target grit level then seal.

## Field Tips

- Tool wear rate tells you everything. Fast wear = insufficient water OR surface still too rough. Slow wear = tool not working effectively.
- The higher your starting Aqua Zero grit, the less conventional resin polishing is required afterwards.
- Residential surface polishes: start at 200 AZ almost always — residential finishes rarely achieve the quality needed for a 400 start.
- Commercial: if the pour was high quality, a 400 AZ start is often achievable.
