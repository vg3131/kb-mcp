# ROX Series Metal Bond Diamonds

The ROX series are Worx+ metal bond grinding segments for the Redi-Loc system. Available in Extra Output (XO) and Extra Long Life (XL) variants across soft, medium, hard and super-hard bonds.

## Tag

Redi-Loc · Metal Bond

## Steps

### Step 1: Select correct bond

Match bond hardness inversely to concrete hardness. Soft concrete = hard bond. Hard concrete = soft bond. Over-hard bond on soft floor causes diamonds to load and stop cutting.

### Step 2: Attach to Redi-Loc disc

Twist-lock segments onto the Redi-Loc holder — no tools, no bolts. Full set of 9 changes in under 2 minutes. Ensure each segment clicks firmly into position.

### Step 3: Set water flow

Mist water 5–10m ahead of the machine. More water on hard/burnished surfaces. Use Cut Sikk on capped burnished slabs where water alone is insufficient.

### Step 4: Monitor dust output

Aim for a bag change every 10–12 minutes. If bags are lasting longer you are losing productivity. Adjust RPM or water before assuming the tool is wrong.

### Step 5: Dressing

If segments load (stop cutting), dress with a dressing block or run over a coarse abrasive surface. Never discard a loaded segment — it can be recovered.

## Field Tips

- Singles = higher point pressure, more aggressive. Doubles = spread load, more passes. Match to available horsepower.
- ROX Extra Output segments on hard bond deliver maximum cut rate on soft to medium concrete.
- If your bags are going 20+ minutes you are not cutting — diagnose bond, RPM or water first.
