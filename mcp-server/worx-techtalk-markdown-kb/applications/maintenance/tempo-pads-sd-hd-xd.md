# Tempo Pads (SD/HD/XD)

Tempo diamond impregnated pads (DIPs) are large-surface-area nylon-backed pads with diamond resin media on the working face. Originally for maintenance, they have expanded to scratch correction, gloss enhancement, surface finishing and high-speed burnishing. The XD variant has a denser matrix, higher surface area and outperforms traditional resins at equivalent grits.

## Tag

Polishing · Maintenance · DIP

## Steps

### Step 1: Coloured side goes down

The coloured side is the working face — diamond media is applied here. Running the wrong side down does nothing. This is the most common early mistake.

### Step 2: Use super rippers for velcro grip

Always use super ripper backing pads with Tempo pads. The grip is dramatically better than standard velcro — pads stay on at higher speeds and under heavier machines.

### Step 3: Scratch correction (lower grits)

At 400 grit Tempo, run over a 200 grit resin finish to remove machine marks and profiling scratches and achieve a clean satin. This is faster than going back to a resin step.

### Step 4: Gloss enhancement (higher grits)

At 800, 1500, 3000, 5000 (XD) grit, use Tempo pads for final gloss levels. XD range delivers higher gloss at lower grits than traditional resins — comparable to running a grit level higher.

### Step 5: Floor maintenance

Finish the job by recommending client maintains the floor with a Tempo pad at the same grit the floor was completed at. A polivac/burnishing machine with a Tempo pad extends floor durability for years.

## Field Tips

- Large working surface area = keeps temperature and weight down. This enables high-speed processing on planetary grinders and burnishing machines.
- XD = Extra Duty. Extra density backing pad, revamped CGT diamond media, higher gloss at lower grits, available up to 5000 grit. Run them faster and in the same direction unlike traditional resins.
- Always insist clients maintain with Tempo pads. The ongoing maintenance recommendation creates repeat supply orders and protects the floor warranty.
