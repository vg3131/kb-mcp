# Totally Repair Cementitious (Grey + White)

High-performance cement-based patching compounds available in grey and white. For repairing cracks, holes, porous surfaces and decorative saw-cut grooves across standard and light/white concrete. Both share the same properties — dry strength, hardening, and polishability. Prime with Totally Restore before applying.

## Tag

Repair · Cementitious · Patchwork

## Steps

### Step 1: Select grey or white

Match to the concrete colour. Grey for standard concrete. White for light, white or off-white concrete mixes, terrazzo or decorative applications. Both polish to the same quality finish.

### Step 2: Prime with Totally Restore

For best results, prime all repair areas with Totally Restore grouting compound first. This improves adhesion and draws the patching compound into the repair area more effectively.

### Step 3: Mix to desired consistency

Mix with water using a drill mixer. Thicker/drier mix = faster dry + harder cure. Wetter mix = more workable but longer cure time. Choose based on project conditions.

### Step 4: Apply to repair area

Apply using a spatula. Work the material into cracks, holes, porous areas or saw-cut grooves. Slightly overfill to allow for grinding back to flush.

### Step 5: Cure 2–6 hours then grind

Allow 2–6 hours cure time (temperature and humidity dependent). Once hardened, grind back to flush with surrounding concrete and polish through your standard resin progression.

## Field Tips

- Always prime with Totally Restore first — adhesion is significantly better.
- White product is a perfect complement to the grey — same properties, different colour. No process changes required when switching between the two.
- Polishability is the critical property for a cementitious repair product. The Totally Repair range is formulated to grind and polish to the same finish as the surrounding concrete.
