# Totally Defend Pen-X (Outdoor)

Pen-X is a single-pack solvent-based outdoor concrete sealer with UV resistance — specifically designed for protecting outdoor concrete surfaces from dirty breasts, mould, algae and UV degradation. Natural colour enhancement without the typical "wet look." Single component makes application easy with high coverage rates.

## Tag

Sealer · Outdoor · UV

## Steps

### Step 1: Confirm outdoor application

Pen-X is specifically for outdoor concrete surfaces. For indoor mechanically polished concrete, use Totally Defend standard formulation. Pen-X provides UV protection that indoor products do not require.

### Step 2: Prepare the surface

Surface must be dry, clean, and free of loose material, efflorescence or previous coatings. Concrete must be cured — Pen-X has increased moisture tolerance on green concrete, but allow minimum cure time for best results.

### Step 3: Apply by roller

Single-component formula is easy to roll. Maintain a wet edge throughout application to avoid lap marks. Apply at the coverage rate specified on the product — do not over-thin. Fast recoat time (often within 30 minutes) allows rapid second coat application.

### Step 4: Allow to cure

Allow full cure before foot traffic. Check product data sheet for specific cure times based on temperature and humidity.

## Field Tips

- High coverage rate and easy wet-edge maintenance make Pen-X the most efficient outdoor sealer for production work.
- UV resistance means the concrete appearance is maintained long-term — especially important for exposed aggregate and premium external finishes.
- The unique solvent blend minimises webbing or flossing during application in warmer weather — a common problem with other outdoor sealers.
