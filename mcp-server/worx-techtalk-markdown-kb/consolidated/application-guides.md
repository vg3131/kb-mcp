# Chemistry


## Totally Boost Potassium + Lithium

Totally Boost is a two-product densifier system — Potassium for mid-stage applications (grouting through to 200 grit) and Lithium for final-stage applications (200 grit through to completion). Formulated off 25+ years in the concrete polishing industry and integrated to work with all Worx+ honing, polishing, grout and sealer products.


Steps:
1. Apply Potassium after grouting: After GRT grouting step, apply Totally Boost Potassium by spray to point of saturation. Keep surface wet for at least 15 minutes. On modern concrete, cure for at least 1 hour after the surface is dry. On old or compromised concrete, allow an overnight cure.
2. Apply Potassium lick coats between resin stages: Apply a lighter lick coat of Potassium prior to each consecutive honing resin (50, 100 grit). Spray application, keep the surface slightly wet while running the resin. This maximises surface hardening and colour uniformity.
3. Switch to Lithium at 200 grit stage: At the 200 grit stage, switch from Potassium to Lithium. Apply by sprayer, spread with a microfibre mop. The surface is now too closed for Potassium — Lithium's smaller molecule penetrates where Potassium cannot.
4. Apply Lithium — as soon as dry, polish: As soon as the Lithium is dry to touch, continue polishing. Unlike Potassium, Lithium does not need an extended cure window. It increases surface integrity and density resulting in better reflectivity and colour correction.


Tips:
- Point of saturation = surface buildup of excess product. This confirms pores are fully loaded. Without visible buildup you cannot confirm maximum penetration.
- Potassium is a mid-stage formula. Lithium is a final-stage formula. Using Potassium beyond 200 grit is wasted product — the pores are already closed.
- Both products formulated to integrate specifically with Worx+ honing and polishing tools — the chemistry and tooling work together.

## Totally Restore (GRT Grouting)

Totally Restore is the Worx+ grouting compound used with GRT metal bond segments to simultaneously grind and fill the pores and voids exposed by the initial metal grind. Pre-wetting the floor before grouting is critical — it changes how the product absorbs and bonds.


Steps:
1. Pre-wet the floor: Before applying Totally Restore, wet the floor with clean water. The pre-wet creates capillary action — it draws the grouting compound deeper into the concrete. A dry concrete surface will not absorb the product the same way. This step is non-negotiable.
2. Prepare Totally Restore: Mix Totally Restore with hot water to your desired consistency. Thicker mix = faster dry, harder cure. The product changes colour as it absorbs — watch for the colour change as your guide.
3. Apply and broom out: Apply Totally Restore to the floor and spread evenly with a broom. Uniform coverage without puddles. The GRT will work it into the floor — your job is to distribute it evenly ahead of the machine.
4. Run GRT segments while wet: Apply GRT segments to the machine and run through the product while it is still wet. The optimal time to work Totally Restore is when it turns blue — this is when it is sticky and has absorbed moisture and lined the pores. Work the machine at a steady, slow pace.
5. Reactivate if needed: If the product starts to dry too fast, spray additional water on top — this reactivates it. Do not discard or skip — reactivation is easy and free.
6. Allow to cure, then densify: After GRT grouting is complete, allow the floor to cure. Then apply Totally Boost Potassium as the first densifier step. The grouted + densified surface is now ready for resin polishing.


Tips:
- Pre-wet one side of a sponge with water, apply Totally Restore to both sides — the water side absorbs product instantly. The dry side repels it. The same principle applies to concrete.
- The colour change (turning blue) indicates the product has loaded the pores and is at optimal working consistency — this is the moment to run the GRT through it.
- Totally Restore also activates dormant unreacted cement in the slab surface — this is why grouted floors are consistently harder than ungrouted ones.

## Totally Cut Sikk

Cut Sikk is a grinding accelerator designed to keep diamonds cool and introduce abrasion to difficult surfaces — enabling hard bond diamonds to cut through burnished, capped or hard concrete that would normally wear soft bonds quickly. A team leader kit covers 100–200 sqm depending on material removal required.


Steps:
1. Wet the floor with water first: Always wet the floor with water before applying Cut Sikk. The water provides initial cooling and helps the product distribute evenly.
2. Mix and spread: Mix the Cut Sikk product and spread it over the floor surface with a broom ahead of the machine. The active particles you can see in the mix create the abrasion — the rest is for cooling.
3. Run hard bond diamonds through product: Run the machine with hard bond segments through the Cut Sikk while the surface is still wet. The combination of water cooling and Cut Sikk abrasion particles allows hard bonds to cut burnished surfaces at walking pace. This is the same approach that completed 8,000+ sqm with one set of segments with 7mm remaining.
4. Reactivate as needed: Cut Sikk can be reactivated — simply spray water over it to reactivate the product. No waste, no re-application needed if you act quickly.


Tips:
- The active abrasion particles in Cut Sikk are what make hard bonds viable on difficult surfaces. Water cools. Particles cut. Together they replace the need for soft bonds.
- Can be used wet or dry — wet application provides better coverage and tool cooling.
- Originally developed for road saws. Adapted for concrete polishing applications.

# Grinding Tools


## ROX Series Metal Bond Diamonds

The ROX series are Worx+ metal bond grinding segments for the Redi-Loc system. Available in Extra Output (XO) and Extra Long Life (XL) variants across soft, medium, hard and super-hard bonds.


Steps:
1. Select correct bond: Match bond hardness inversely to concrete hardness. Soft concrete = hard bond. Hard concrete = soft bond. Over-hard bond on soft floor causes diamonds to load and stop cutting.
2. Attach to Redi-Loc disc: Twist-lock segments onto the Redi-Loc holder — no tools, no bolts. Full set of 9 changes in under 2 minutes. Ensure each segment clicks firmly into position.
3. Set water flow: Mist water 5–10m ahead of the machine. More water on hard/burnished surfaces. Use Cut Sikk on capped burnished slabs where water alone is insufficient.
4. Monitor dust output: Aim for a bag change every 10–12 minutes. If bags are lasting longer you are losing productivity. Adjust RPM or water before assuming the tool is wrong.
5. Dressing: If segments load (stop cutting), dress with a dressing block or run over a coarse abrasive surface. Never discard a loaded segment — it can be recovered.


Tips:
- Singles = higher point pressure, more aggressive. Doubles = spread load, more passes. Match to available horsepower.
- ROX Extra Output segments on hard bond deliver maximum cut rate on soft to medium concrete.
- If your bags are going 20+ minutes you are not cutting — diagnose bond, RPM or water first.

## Shredda PCD

The Shredda PCD is a new-generation PCD tool featuring five truncated inverted PCD segments uniquely arranged for shredding medium-to-thick surface contaminants — tile adhesive, levelling compound, trowelled-down epoxy — faster than the impact PCD while leaving the substrate cleaner.

## Aqua Zero Floor Pads

Aqua Zeros are wet-process surface polish tools (50–400 grit) designed to clean and prepare a burnished or trowelled concrete surface for a nil-exposure finish. "Aqua" = used wet, "Zero" = minimal stock removal. Most critical factor: selecting the correct starting grit based on the existing surface texture.


Steps:
1. Assess surface texture: This is the most important step. If the finish is high-quality power-trowelled concrete, start at 400 Aqua Zero. If the finish is borderline or rougher, start at 200 Aqua Zero to protect the 400 from wearing prematurely. Do not skip this assessment.
2. Set water delivery: Water must go directly into the back of the machine — NOT pooling in front. Good skirt seal = water pressure maintained under the machine, keeping tools cool and flushing slurry. Target 6–10 litres per minute.
3. Clean the surface first: Remove all dust, debris and grit before running Aqua Zeros. Any particles under the machine will scratch the surface — on a surface polish there is nowhere to hide mistakes.
4. Run 200 Aqua Zero (if required): Removes surface texture, making the 400 more effective and extending its life. Inspect the surface after each pass. The goal is a consistent, slightly opened texture across the whole floor.
5. Run 400 Aqua Zero: Refines the surface. Monitor tool wear regularly — if wearing too fast, increase water or check surface prep. Consistent speed and overlapping passes are essential.
6. Clean and apply Lithium Boost: Thoroughly clean the floor after Aqua Zero work. Apply Totally Boost Lithium via microfibre. Allow to dry fully.
7. Transition to resins: If you started at 400 Aqua Zero, begin resins at 100 or 200 Universal. If you started at 200 AZ, begin at 100 Universal. Finish to target grit level then seal.


Tips:
- Tool wear rate tells you everything. Fast wear = insufficient water OR surface still too rough. Slow wear = tool not working effectively.
- The higher your starting Aqua Zero grit, the less conventional resin polishing is required afterwards.
- Residential surface polishes: start at 200 AZ almost always — residential finishes rarely achieve the quality needed for a 400 start.
- Commercial: if the pour was high quality, a 400 AZ start is often achievable.

# Maintenance


## Totally Care Floor Cleaner

Totally Care is a pH-neutral floor cleaner free from bases, phosphates and gloss agents. Formulated specifically for mechanically polished concrete — does not leave residues that attract dust or accelerate surface wear. Use in combination with regular Tempo pad buffing for a complete maintenance system.


Steps:
1. Clear debris first: Sweep or vacuum the floor to remove dust and debris before mopping. Cleaning over loose particles will scratch the surface and drag dirt across the floor.
2. Dilute correctly: For every 10 litres of water, add 20–40ml of Totally Care. Do not use undiluted — the concentrated product is designed for this dilution ratio.
3. Mop or scrub: Apply using a mop and bucket or industrial floor scrubbing machine. Totally Care is compatible with both methods. Work in sections, overlapping each pass.
4. Allow to dry: Allow the floor to dry naturally. No rinsing required at the dilution rate specified.
5. Buff with Tempo pad: Combine regular Totally Care cleaning with periodic buffing using a Tempo pad at the same grit the floor was finished at. This is the complete maintenance system that protects the floor long-term.


Tips:
- The absence of gloss agents is deliberate — gloss agents in regular floor cleaners build up on polished surfaces and accelerate wear.
- Regular cleaning + Tempo buffing is not just maintenance — it is the system that keeps clients happy long after handover and generates referrals.
- Always specify Totally Care to clients at handover. Never let a client clean polished concrete with whatever they happen to have on hand.

## Tempo Pads (SD/HD/XD)

Tempo diamond impregnated pads (DIPs) are large-surface-area nylon-backed pads with diamond resin media on the working face. Originally for maintenance, they have expanded to scratch correction, gloss enhancement, surface finishing and high-speed burnishing. The XD variant has a denser matrix, higher surface area and outperforms traditional resins at equivalent grits.


Steps:
1. Coloured side goes down: The coloured side is the working face — diamond media is applied here. Running the wrong side down does nothing. This is the most common early mistake.
2. Use super rippers for velcro grip: Always use super ripper backing pads with Tempo pads. The grip is dramatically better than standard velcro — pads stay on at higher speeds and under heavier machines.
3. Scratch correction (lower grits): At 400 grit Tempo, run over a 200 grit resin finish to remove machine marks and profiling scratches and achieve a clean satin. This is faster than going back to a resin step.
4. Gloss enhancement (higher grits): At 800, 1500, 3000, 5000 (XD) grit, use Tempo pads for final gloss levels. XD range delivers higher gloss at lower grits than traditional resins — comparable to running a grit level higher.
5. Floor maintenance: Finish the job by recommending client maintains the floor with a Tempo pad at the same grit the floor was completed at. A polivac/burnishing machine with a Tempo pad extends floor durability for years.


Tips:
- Large working surface area = keeps temperature and weight down. This enables high-speed processing on planetary grinders and burnishing machines.
- XD = Extra Duty. Extra density backing pad, revamped CGT diamond media, higher gloss at lower grits, available up to 5000 grit. Run them faster and in the same direction unlike traditional resins.
- Always insist clients maintain with Tempo pads. The ongoing maintenance recommendation creates repeat supply orders and protects the floor warranty.

# Repairs


## Totally Repair Resin (Two-Part)

Totally Repair Resin is a two-part fast-curing resin for small cracks, spalls and holes. Key principle: always use native aggregate materials (from the actual slab) mixed with the resin for best colour match. Break all repair edges to create a natural profile and maximise mechanical adhesion.


Steps:
1. Prepare the repair area: Break in the edges of cracks or spalls to create a natural, non-straight profile — this helps the repair blend visually and creates a stronger mechanical bond. Straight, saw-cut edges look like repairs; natural edges blend.
2. Collect native aggregate: Collect small pieces of the actual concrete being repaired. Mix these with the resin (Part A) before adding the hardener (Part B). Native materials produce dramatically better colour matching than importing foreign aggregate.
3. Mix Part A + aggregate first: Combine aggregate with Part A resin and get the consistency right before adding Part B. Once Part B is added, the clock starts — 5–10 minute working window. Adding Part B too early wastes material.
4. Apply and allow to cure: Fill the repair area. At 20°C, expect 45 minutes to dry up nice and hard. Surface may be slightly sticky on top — this is normal. Do not disturb until fully cured.
5. Grind back and polish: Once set, grind the repair back flush with the surrounding floor surface. Polish through your normal resin progression to the same finish level as the surrounding floor.


Tips:
- Native materials vs foreign: the difference in colour match is dramatic. Always collect material from the actual slab being repaired.
- A strong mechanical adhesion property means the resin bonds aggressively — but the edge prep is still important for visual integration.
- Two-part fast-curing resin is excellent for short time-framed projects where overnight repairs are not possible.

## Totally Repair Redi-Fix

The Redi-Fix is a one-component fast-setting repair product in a cartridge — cementitious/resin blend, ready to go with just a pull of the trigger. No mixing, no tools. Self-mixing nozzle delivers uniform material directly into the repair area. Sets in 20–30 minutes.


Steps:
1. Prepare the repair area: Surface must be dry, clean and free of loose material. For cracks and holes, remove any crumbling or loose concrete. For broken pieces, ensure broken faces are clean.
2. Prime the nozzle: Fit the self-mixing nozzle to the cartridge and load into the dispensing gun. Dispense a small amount onto scrap cardboard and allow material to emerge until a uniform colour is achieved — this purges any unmixed material from the nozzle.
3. Apply: For holes, saw cuts and small cracks: apply Redi-Fix directly into the repair area. For broken pieces: apply liberally to both faces to adhere back together. For deeper damage: mix in native aggregate for a more uniform result and colour match.
4. Colour matching: Mix tints with the Redi-Fix material if colour matching is required. Work within a 5–10 minute window after mixing tints before setting begins.
5. Allow to set then process: Expect 20–30 minutes to set after application. Once set, follow your normal flooring process to achieve the desired finish.


Tips:
- No mixing, no additional tools. This is the fastest repair method in the Worx+ range — ideal for on-site, on-the-day fixes.
- The self-mixing nozzle is the key — two components mixed at the nozzle ensures correct ratio every time without manual mixing.
- For deeper concrete damage, mixing in native aggregate improves the final visual result significantly.

## Totally Repair Cementitious (Grey + White)

High-performance cement-based patching compounds available in grey and white. For repairing cracks, holes, porous surfaces and decorative saw-cut grooves across standard and light/white concrete. Both share the same properties — dry strength, hardening, and polishability. Prime with Totally Restore before applying.


Steps:
1. Select grey or white: Match to the concrete colour. Grey for standard concrete. White for light, white or off-white concrete mixes, terrazzo or decorative applications. Both polish to the same quality finish.
2. Prime with Totally Restore: For best results, prime all repair areas with Totally Restore grouting compound first. This improves adhesion and draws the patching compound into the repair area more effectively.
3. Mix to desired consistency: Mix with water using a drill mixer. Thicker/drier mix = faster dry + harder cure. Wetter mix = more workable but longer cure time. Choose based on project conditions.
4. Apply to repair area: Apply using a spatula. Work the material into cracks, holes, porous areas or saw-cut grooves. Slightly overfill to allow for grinding back to flush.
5. Cure 2–6 hours then grind: Allow 2–6 hours cure time (temperature and humidity dependent). Once hardened, grind back to flush with surrounding concrete and polish through your standard resin progression.


Tips:
- Always prime with Totally Restore first — adhesion is significantly better.
- White product is a perfect complement to the grey — same properties, different colour. No process changes required when switching between the two.
- Polishability is the critical property for a cementitious repair product. The Totally Repair range is formulated to grind and polish to the same finish as the surrounding concrete.

# Sealers


## Totally Defend (Solvent vs Water Based)

Totally Defend impregnating sealers come in both solvent-based and water-based formulations. Both perform excellently on mechanically polished concrete, stone and terrazzo. The objective: maintain spills at the top surface level, allowing cleaning without permanent damage. Neither is a topical barrier — they work from inside the concrete to protect it.


Steps:
1. Assess conditions: Check ambient temperature, surface temperature and relative humidity. Both variants need 15–25°C and below 75–80% RH. If there are known moisture issues in the slab or high ambient humidity, choose the water-based formulation — it is significantly less sensitive to moisture than the solvent-based product.
2. Warm the product (solvent): In cool conditions, solvents separate from solids inside the tin. The product looks normal but will not penetrate correctly. Warm the tin in the sun or a bucket of warm water before opening — this reactivates the molecules and restores correct viscosity.
3. Clean the floor: Sweep and dry mop the floor thoroughly. Remove any polishing residue, dust or contamination. Any material between the sealer and the concrete will affect penetration.
4. Apply in thin, even coats: Apply via microfibre mop in light, even coats — 1 to 2 coats maximum. Thin application is critical. Over-application creates a surface film that cannot cure correctly and will haze.
5. Buff within the application window: Once dry (approx. 30–40 minutes), buff the entire floor with a white or blue pad on a burnisher or polivac. Buffing is not optional — it drives the sealer in and removes any excess that remains on the surface.


Tips:
- Solvent-based reapplication cycle: 5–8 years. Water-based: 3–5 years. Choose based on project conditions and client expectations.
- An impregnating sealer limits damage to the top surface only — if a stain occurs, a technician can re-polish just the affected area and re-apply sealer without replacing the floor.
- If the sealer hazes (blooms), the surface was too cold, too wet, or too much product was applied. See the Sealer Failure restoration methods in the Troubleshooting section.

## Totally Defend Pen-X (Outdoor)

Pen-X is a single-pack solvent-based outdoor concrete sealer with UV resistance — specifically designed for protecting outdoor concrete surfaces from dirty breasts, mould, algae and UV degradation. Natural colour enhancement without the typical "wet look." Single component makes application easy with high coverage rates.


Steps:
1. Confirm outdoor application: Pen-X is specifically for outdoor concrete surfaces. For indoor mechanically polished concrete, use Totally Defend standard formulation. Pen-X provides UV protection that indoor products do not require.
2. Prepare the surface: Surface must be dry, clean, and free of loose material, efflorescence or previous coatings. Concrete must be cured — Pen-X has increased moisture tolerance on green concrete, but allow minimum cure time for best results.

4. Allow to cure: Allow full cure before foot traffic. Check product data sheet for specific cure times based on temperature and humidity.


Tips:

- UV resistance means the concrete appearance is maintained long-term — especially important for exposed aggregate and premium external finishes.
