# Contractors Are Manufacturers — Own Your Brand

## Quick Answer

Installing other companies' specified systems builds their brand, not yours. See yourself as a manufacturer with your own product. Clients should call for YOUR brand. Your reputation is your specification.

> You're not a contracting business — you're a manufacturer. Build your brand.
