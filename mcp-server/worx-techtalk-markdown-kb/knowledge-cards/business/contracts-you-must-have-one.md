# Contracts: You Must Have One

## Quick Answer

A written contract protects both parties. Key elements: scope of work, exclusions (what the quote doesn't cover), payment terms, what constitutes completion, and a clear process for defect identification. The most dangerous phrase in the industry: 'she'll be right, I trust them.' That trust disappears when there's money in dispute.

## Source Quote

> Setting yourself up for success — stand firm on a few things. A contract is not about distrust. It's about clarity.
