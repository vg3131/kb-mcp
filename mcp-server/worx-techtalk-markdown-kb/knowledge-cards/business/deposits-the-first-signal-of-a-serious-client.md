# Deposits: The First Signal of a Serious Client

## Quick Answer

If a client argues over a deposit, the full payment at the end will be even harder to collect. Deposits cover your materials and labour cost upfront, protect you from cancellations, and are a genuine test of client commitment. Milan's rule from his contracting days: if they won't pay 15–30% upfront to cover costs, the job isn't worth taking.

## Source Quote

> One of the biggest changes I made was deposits. If they're ironing about a deposit, how difficult will it be to capture 100% at the end?
