# Focus During Uncertain Times: Choose Abundance, Not Scarcity

## Quick Answer

In uncertain market cycles, contractors fall into a scarcity mindset — cutting prices, panicking about pipeline, watching competitors. The alternative is an abundance mindset: focus on what you can control (quality, productivity, marketing, client relationships). The sky-is-falling calls always come in waves, but the operators who focus on building rather than surviving come out stronger on the other side.

## Source Quote

> Uncertainty happens all the time in business. The things you focus on are the things that get tended to. Choose abundance.
