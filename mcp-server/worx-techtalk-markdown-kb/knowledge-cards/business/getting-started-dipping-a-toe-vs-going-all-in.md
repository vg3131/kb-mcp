# Getting Started: Dipping a Toe vs Going All In

## Quick Answer

Two approaches to entering the industry: (1) Dip a toe — start with a single-phase machine, do residential and small commercial work, build knowledge and cashflow. (2) Go all in — buy a three-phase setup, hire from day one, target commercial. The right choice depends on your capital position, risk tolerance, and existing client base. Either way, training and system knowledge should come before the machine purchase — not after.

## Source Quote

> The difference between getting your feet wet and going in full tilt — what are the real world costs, what competition will you face, what advice would you give? It's not a short answer.
