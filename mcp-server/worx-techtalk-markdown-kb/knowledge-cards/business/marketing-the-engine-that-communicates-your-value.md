# Marketing: The Engine That Communicates Your Value

## Quick Answer

Marketing is the engine that communicates the value your business delivers and connects you with the right people (your target market). It's not just social media — it includes your vehicle, your email signature, how you answer the phone, your quoting documents, and the photos you share. If your marketing materials look budget, you attract budget clients. A consistent, quality brand presence across all touchpoints is marketing.

## Source Quote

> Marketing is an engine that communicates the value the company is here to bring. It connects you with the right people — your target market.
