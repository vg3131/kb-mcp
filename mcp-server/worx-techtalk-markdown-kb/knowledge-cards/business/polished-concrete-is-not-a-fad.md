# Polished Concrete Is Not a Fad

## Quick Answer

Josh Van der Veen has been asked for 20 years whether polished concrete is a fad. Answer: polished concrete was one of the first finishes adopted in major commercial spaces, has grown steadily for 25+ years, and is now specified in Green Star buildings globally. The fads that have come and gone around it — epoxy flake, overlays, quartz coatings — have not replaced it.

## Source Quote

> What other fads have been around for 20 years? Polished concrete is very much here to stay.
