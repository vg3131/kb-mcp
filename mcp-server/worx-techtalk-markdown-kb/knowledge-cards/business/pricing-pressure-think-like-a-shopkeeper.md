# Pricing Pressure: Think Like a Shopkeeper

## Quick Answer

When diesel, labour, tooling, or any other input cost rises, your price must go up to maintain margin. Treat each cost element like a product on a shelf — if the supplier increases their price, yours must increase too. Most contractors absorb rising costs silently until they're no longer viable. Review your base rates at the start of every year.

## Source Quote

> I imagine it as a shopkeeper selling products. If my product's cost goes up, the price on my shelf goes up. That's all it is.
