# Project Staging: 80% of Problems Come From Unclear Expectations

## Quick Answer

About 80% of the phone calls Works Plus receives trace back to unclear expectations between contractor and client. Define who is responsible at each stage — first grind, lock-up, final polish — in writing. Who covers damage caused by other trades? What happens if the concrete isn't what was specified? Setting this out before starting eliminates most disputes.

## Source Quote

> It comes from not having a clear idea of expectations alignment. That is the root cause of about 80% of the phone calls we get.
