# Protecting Your Bottom Line: Measure Labour

## Quick Answer

Labour is the biggest variable in your cost structure. If your crew isn't set up with the right tools and processes, they drag out time. Track sqm per hour for each operator — if one person consistently does 20% less, find out why. Productivity measurement protects margin and identifies training gaps before they become financial problems.

## Source Quote

> Labor is a killer. If your guys aren't set up for success, labour drags out. Measure productivity off your guys on site.
