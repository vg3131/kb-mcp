# Samples and Mock-Ups: Non-Negotiable on New Sites

## Quick Answer

On sites where you haven't seen the concrete beforehand, know nothing about the placement, and have no idea what the project history is — do a sample. Every time. The sample reveals the process required for the full project, sets the client's visual expectation, and gives you documented evidence of the achievable result before work starts. It's not optional — it's self-protection.

## Source Quote

> On Rock Up jobs, samples and mock-ups — yes. When I get to a job green, knowing very little about the concrete, I do samples on the majority of my floors.
