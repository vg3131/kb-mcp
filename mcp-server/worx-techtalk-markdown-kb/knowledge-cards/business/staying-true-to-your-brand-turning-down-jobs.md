# Staying True to Your Brand: Turning Down Jobs

## Quick Answer

Taking jobs that don't suit your brand damages your reputation faster than no work at all. If someone says 'I don't want anything fancy, I'm paying cash, just something simple' — that's a red flag. A budget job done by a premium operator sets a precedent and almost always leads to a dissatisfied client. Politely decline and refer to someone better suited.

## Source Quote

> If we hear: 'I'm not looking for anything like that, just something budget' — it doesn't align with what Rock Up does. That's an alarm bell.
