# Sustainability & Resilience in the Industry

## Quick Answer

These terms are overused and abused. Real sustainability means being able to answer the phone when builders call next year. Real resilience means having systems that survive market cycles. When approval rates shift, unprepared businesses are the first casualties.

## Source Quote

> Sustainability is ensuring that when builders pick up the phone, your number still rings.
