# The True Cost of Downtime: $120K/Year

## Quick Answer

Losing just 2.5 minutes per hour = 5 sqm/day lost. Over 240 working days = 1,200 sqm = $120,000 at $100/sqm. Eliminating small inefficiencies has an enormous compounding effect on annual revenue.

## Source Quote

> 1,200 square meters a year. All because we lost 2½ minutes every hour.
