# You Don't Know What You Don't Know

## Quick Answer

Long-term industry veterans can be the most resistant to new processes — not because the new method is wrong, but because the old one has 'always worked.' Newcomers often have better marketing, more systematic processes, and faster adoption of new technology. The industry moves forward fastest when experienced operators stay curious and new operators respect the depth of experience already in the market.

## Source Quote

> Some of us have been in the industry a long time — and maybe not necessarily moving with the times. And newcomers are doing impressive things.
