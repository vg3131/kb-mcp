# Cold Joints: The Defect That Goes to the Bone

## Quick Answer

Cold joints from delayed pours create visible colour, texture and aggregate inconsistencies. They're structural — polishing can't fix them. Nothing is documented on pour day, so polishers discover them too late.

## Source Quote

> A cold joint goes to the bone. Beauty is only skin deep — ugly goes to the bone.
