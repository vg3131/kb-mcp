# Fibre Reinforced Concrete: What It Means for Polishing

## Quick Answer

Fibre reinforced concrete (steel or polypropylene fibres) is increasingly common in industrial slabs. Steel fibres can appear at the surface during grinding — they're usually manageable. Polypropylene fibres melt during trowelling and create a microporosity that affects densifier absorption. Identify whether fibres are present before quoting and factor in additional densifier application.

## Source Quote

> Fibres are becoming more common. Steel fibres at the surface are manageable — but polypropylene fibres change how the surface absorbs everything you put on it.
