# Oils in Floors: You Must Identify Before Quoting

## Quick Answer

Oil contamination in concrete (machine shops, kitchens, old factories) can run 30+ years deep. A small oil stain visible on a site visit may be the tip of an iceberg. If you proceed without disclosing what you found, you own the problem. Always do a thorough site inspection, scratch test a few spots, and document any contamination in your quote. Remediation options include heat extraction, poultice drawing, and specific chemical degreasers.

## Source Quote

> I did a site visit and saw a small amount of oil. Turns out 200sqm was contaminated with cutting lubricants from a 30-year-old glazier factory.
