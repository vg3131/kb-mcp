# Densifier as Curing Compound Replacement

## Quick Answer

Trial data from a big-box retailer: same-day densifier achieved higher core strength than water curing or standard curing compound. Eliminated calcium carbonate staining. For high-SCM concrete (slag/fly ash), applying densifier immediately after finishing is the smart approach.

## Source Quote

> Densifier cores came back higher strength. No calcium carbonate. The engineer accepted it as the curing method.
