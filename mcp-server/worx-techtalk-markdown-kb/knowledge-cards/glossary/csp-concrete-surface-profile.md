# CSP — Concrete Surface Profile

## Quick Answer

CSP (Concrete Surface Profile) is the standardised measurement of surface texture/roughness after preparation. CSP 1–3 is suitable for thin coatings and penetrating sealers. CSP 3–5 suits epoxy and thicker coatings. CSP 6–9 is used for trowelled-on overlays. Mechanical grinding typically produces CSP 2–4 depending on diamond grit. Know the required CSP before specifying a finish.

## Source Quote

> CSP is the language that connects surface preparation to coating adhesion. If a coating fails, CSP is the first thing to verify.
