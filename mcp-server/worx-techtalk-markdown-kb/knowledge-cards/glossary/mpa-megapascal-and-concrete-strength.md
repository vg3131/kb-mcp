# MPA — Megapascal and Concrete Strength

## Quick Answer

MPA (megapascal) is the measure of concrete compressive strength. Standard residential slab: 20–25 MPA. Standard commercial: 32 MPA. High performance: 40–50 MPA. Higher MPA means denser concrete with less free lime and less porosity. This affects densifier absorption rate, tool bond selection, and how fast the floor responds to each polishing stage.

## Source Quote

> Higher MPA, denser concrete, less free lime. Your densifier process needs to compensate — more applications, heavier concentration.
