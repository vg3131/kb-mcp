# P-Rating — Slip Resistance Classification

## Quick Answer

P-ratings (AS 4586) classify slip resistance of floor surfaces tested wet. P1: low risk (dry-only areas). P2: low risk (covered areas). P3: moderate risk (covered external, car parks). P4: high risk (external areas exposed to weather). P5: highest risk (pool surrounds, fish markets). Mechanically polished concrete at 400 grit typically achieves P3–P4 wet. Burnished finishes are lower. Always test and document.

## Source Quote

> Know your P-rating. If someone slips and you haven't documented it, that falls on you.
