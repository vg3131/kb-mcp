# Designer Crete: The $250K Lesson

## Quick Answer

An on-site batch-mixed cementitious overlay using specially sourced aggregates and white binder (SDT). Nearly won Federation Square. The supply was pulled due to a personal feud. Cost ~$250K in the 1990s and almost ended the business. Led directly to the pivot to mechanically polished concrete.

## Source Quote

> Designer Crete nearly cost us the business. Very unattractive, very quickly.
