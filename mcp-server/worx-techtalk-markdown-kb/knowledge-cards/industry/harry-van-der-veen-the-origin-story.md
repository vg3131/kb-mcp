# Harry Van der Veen: The Origin Story

## Quick Answer

Born Rotterdam, arrived Melbourne 1968. Started from terrazzo grinding in shopping centres. When the HTC relationship ended, Tim invented dual-drive and ProGrind was born. The densifier (Hyper Floor) came from discussions about hardening free lime in concrete — inspired by 'water glass' used on Dutch sand dunes.

## Source Quote

> We had to make something to harden the free lime. Water glass gave us the direction for the densifier.
