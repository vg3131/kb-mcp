# Josh Jones — STI / Prep Master (USA)

## Quick Answer

First international guest on the podcast. Josh Jones's father started Surface Dimensions in 1991 as a contracting firm in Chicago. By 1995 they saw the processes could be sold to other contractors — STI was born. Known globally as Prep Master. Represents the deep roots of concrete preparation as an industry in North America.
