# Justin Blankenship & the Badass Community

## Quick Answer

Justin runs the Badass Concrete Polishers & Decorative Concrete Masters Facebook group — likely the largest online community for the industry. Second-generation concreter from Maryland, USA. Bridges US and Australian knowledge. His community is where many contractors first share problems (and get conflicting advice — hence Kurt's 'blinkers on' philosophy).
