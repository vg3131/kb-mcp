# Cold Weather: Protecting Machinery and Products

## Quick Answer

In cold and wet conditions: solvent-based sealers separate — always warm product before use. Water-based products perform better in moisture-prone environments, so consider switching in winter. For machinery, moisture in motors is the primary cold-weather failure cause. Store machines indoors, check electrical connections after rain, and don't pressure-wash cold machines. Vacuum hoses that have been cold and wet are vulnerable — check seals and connections before each use.

## Source Quote

> Solvent-based impregnating sealer — this time of year the effects of cold weather and condensation are a real issue. The water-based product works a lot better in moisture scenarios.
