# Dust Output = Your Productivity KPI

## Quick Answer

Use dust bag change frequency as your benchmark. Know your 10-minute bag rate on your old machine, then aim to match or beat it on new equipment. Bags per hour = stock removal = revenue.

## Source Quote

> If I'm doing 10-minute bags on my 680, I want to find that zone on my new machine.
