# How to Clean Your Machine Properly

## Quick Answer

Never pressure wash a concrete grinder with high-pressure water — it drives moisture into the motor housing, bearings, and electronics. Use a stiff brush or compressed air to remove dried slurry. For the underside plate, a flat scraper is the best tool. Clean after every use to prevent slurry from hardening around the head and causing head wobble.

## Source Quote

> Do I take it to the local servo and pressure wash it? No — that's the worst thing you can do. Moisture into the motor is a death sentence.
