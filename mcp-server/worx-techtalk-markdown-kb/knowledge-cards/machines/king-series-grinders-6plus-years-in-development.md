# King Series Grinders: 6+ Years in Development

## Quick Answer

The new King Series grinders (GK) were unveiled at World of Concrete 2025 in Las Vegas. Development started very early in Hayden's tenure at Works Plus — over 6 years of R&D. The machines were still being finalised in December before January shipping. Four machines appeared on the WoC booth.
