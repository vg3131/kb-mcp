# Summer Grinding: Heat, UV and Equipment Protection

## Quick Answer

Summer changes everything on site. UV from direct sunlight degrades vacuum hoses faster than anything else — inspect them regularly. In hot conditions, sealer flashes off before penetrating, densifier dries faster than it should penetrate, and resin tools overheat. Work earlier in the day, keep more water ahead of the machine than usual, and never leave sealers in direct sunlight — warm them to working temperature but don't let them bake.

## Source Quote

> UV is a big issue. The calls I get in summer are based on polishing out in the sun — that concrete gets very hot and changes everything.
