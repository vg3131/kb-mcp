# Aqua Zeros (Micro-Grouting with Speciality Resins)

## Quick Answer

Aqua Zeros are a tooling option for micro-grouting — using a speciality resin-type product to fill very fine surface voids at the resin stage. The key mistake is running them like a standard 50 resin: they are not the same product and won't perform the same way. When used correctly, they can supplement the grouting step for finer pin-hole filling. They don't replace GRT metal-bond grouting — they sit on top of it to refine the surface.

## Source Quote

> Guys are using them to try micro-grouting — to fill up holes with something a bit stronger. But you have to use them correctly, not like a standard 50 specialty.
