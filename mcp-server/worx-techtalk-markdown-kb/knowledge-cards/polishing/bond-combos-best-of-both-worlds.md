# Bond Combos: Best of Both Worlds

## Quick Answer

Combining different bond hardnesses on the same machine gives the best of both worlds. Heavily burnished concrete that looks impermeable might actually have coarse materials underneath — a full set of soft bonds will wear too fast, full hards won't bite. Mix soft and hard bonds to adapt on the fly.

## Source Quote

> The thing about combining bonds is you get the best of both worlds.
