# Corridors and Narrow Spaces: Strategy Matters

## Quick Answer

In corridors under 1,200mm wide, a 680 cuts harder to one side — if the floor isn't perfectly flat, the machine tilts and creates a ridge down the middle. Strategy: ensure the floor is as flat as possible before entering the narrow space, use the machine's natural bias deliberately by alternating direction each pass, and finish with hand grinding to blend. Know the floor profile before committing.

## Source Quote

> Only access to a 680 and only 1200mm wide — the machine cuts harder to one side. Avoid that ridge down the middle at all costs.
