# Cross Cutting and Cross Hatching: When and Why

## Quick Answer

Cross cutting (running a second pass perpendicular to the first) ensures you don't miss areas between passes. Cross hatching at the grouting/densifier stage helps achieve uniform coverage. Both techniques add time but dramatically improve consistency on larger areas. Not every floor needs it — assess your profile first.

## Source Quote

> Cross cutting ensures you're not leaving stripes. Cross hatching at grout stage helps uniformity.
