# Cut Patterns: RC vs Planetary — Understanding the Difference

## Quick Answer

The Works+ RC dual-drive system leaves a different cut profile to planetary (HTC-style) machines. Neither is inherently better — they're different. RC machines leave a directional scratch on burnished floors; planetary machines leave circular patterns. Understanding your machine's cut means you can use it deliberately: cross-cut runs compensate for directional bias, and knowing the pattern at each grit helps you verify it's been fully removed before progressing.

## Source Quote

> Different cuts, you'd think they cut the same. But different cuts on different machines — you need to be wary of them and use them to your benefit.
