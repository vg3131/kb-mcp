# Handover Buff: When and Why

## Quick Answer

The handover buff is a burnishing pass (white or blue pad, burnisher or polivac) done ideally as close to project handover as possible — not when you finish polishing. Doing it weeks before handover means trades contaminate the floor. Doing it the day before handover means the client sees the floor at its best. Frame it as a value-add: you're coming back to ensure the floor is presented perfectly. It's also a natural moment to upsell maintenance programs.

## Source Quote

> The handover buff is an opportunity. The person you've been doing the work for — whether it's a builder or retail client — sees the beautiful finished product right at handover.
