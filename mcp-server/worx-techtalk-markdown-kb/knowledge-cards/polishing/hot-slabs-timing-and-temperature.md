# Hot Slabs: Timing and Temperature

## Quick Answer

Working on hot slabs (fresh concrete in summer or heated indoor environments) changes tool behaviour dramatically. Diamonds overheat faster, densifiers flash off before penetrating, and timing windows for each step compress. Adjust RPM downward, increase water application, and work in shorter runs.

## Source Quote

> Hot slabs change everything — your tool behaviour, your densifier absorption, your timing.
