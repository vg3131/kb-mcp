# How Soon is Too Soon to Polish?

## Quick Answer

Polishing too early (before adequate curing) means the concrete hasn't developed full strength — tools cut too aggressively, densifier penetrates too deep, and shrinkage cracks may still be forming. Minimum 28 days is standard but 56+ days is preferred for best results on residential.

## Source Quote

> If you polish before most of the shrinkage is done, those cracks might open again.
