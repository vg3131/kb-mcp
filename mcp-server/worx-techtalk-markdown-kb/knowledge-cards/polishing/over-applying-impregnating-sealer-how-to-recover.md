# Over-Applying Impregnating Sealer: How to Recover

## Quick Answer

Too much impregnating sealer (Defend or similar) creates a surface film that must be removed before the floor can be further polished or resealed. The usual recovery method is re-grinding back to the last resin stage to physically remove the built-up layer. Prevention: apply in thin, even coats and buff off excess within the application window — don't leave puddles.
