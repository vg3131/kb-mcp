# Surface Polish with Grouting: When It Makes Sense

## Quick Answer

For surface polishes where the concrete has pinholing or minor cracks, a single grouting pass at 200 grit before transitioning to resins can dramatically improve the final appearance. The grout fills surface porosity that would otherwise create dull patches under a sealer. This is not a full grouting process — a targeted lick coat of grout at 200 is enough.

## Source Quote

> Is there an opportunity to get some grouting in if the concrete has pinholing? Yes — a grouting step at 200 before your resin transition makes a real difference.
