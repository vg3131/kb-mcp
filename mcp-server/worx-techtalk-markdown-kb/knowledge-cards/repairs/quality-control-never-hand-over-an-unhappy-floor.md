# Quality Control: Never Hand Over an Unhappy Floor

## Quick Answer

Keep backup tools for every likely failure. Problem-solve systematically. Refuse to deliver below your own standard. The van is your 'treasure trove of solved problems.'

## Source Quote

> If I'm not happy with a floor, I won't hand it over. My van is a treasure trove.
