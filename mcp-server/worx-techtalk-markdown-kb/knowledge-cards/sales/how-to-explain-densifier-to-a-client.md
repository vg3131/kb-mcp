# How to Explain Densifier to a Client

## Quick Answer

Simple language for clients: 'A densifier hardens the top layer of your concrete — it reacts with the calcium in the slab to create a harder surface that polishes better, holds its shine longer, and resists surface abrasion. Think of it as strengthening the concrete from within rather than coating the top.' Never say it makes the floor bulletproof.

## Source Quote

> The simplest way: it reacts with the free lime in the concrete and turns it into something harder. Like turning chalk into stone.
