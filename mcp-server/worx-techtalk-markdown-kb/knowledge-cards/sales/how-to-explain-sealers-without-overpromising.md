# How to Explain Sealers Without Overpromising

## Quick Answer

Tell clients: sealers improve stain resistance and make cleaning easier — they do NOT make concrete impervious. Impregnating sealers (Totally Defend) are invisible and don't change the look — they work inside the concrete. Topical sealers add sheen but can scratch. Nothing protects concrete from oil sitting for 48 hours, strong acids, or sustained abrasion. Manage expectations in writing.

## Source Quote

> I've seen too many floors damaged because the client was told 'the sealer will protect everything.' Tell them the truth.
