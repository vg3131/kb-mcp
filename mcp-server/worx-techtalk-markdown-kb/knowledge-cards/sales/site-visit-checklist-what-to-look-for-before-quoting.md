# Site Visit Checklist: What to Look For Before Quoting

## Quick Answer

Before quoting any polished concrete job: (1) Identify concrete type — residential raft or commercial slab, (2) Check edges — hand-trowelled or power-trowelled, (3) Scratch test hardness in multiple spots, (4) Check for cold joints, cracks, oil stains, or previous coatings, (5) Confirm slab age and any curing compounds applied, (6) Check moisture with plastic sheet test, (7) Photograph everything. Price is based on what you find, not what you're told.

## Source Quote

> Don't quote from photos. A site visit is not optional — it's where you find out what you're actually dealing with.
