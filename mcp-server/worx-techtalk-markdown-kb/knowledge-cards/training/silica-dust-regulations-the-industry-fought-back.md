# Silica Dust Regulations: The Industry Fought Back

## Quick Answer

New silica handling legislation proposed from Canberra would have imposed strict controls on all concrete grinding and polishing work. Works Plus worked with industry bodies to lobby and provide technical input. Key outcome: regulations were refined to be workable for polishers while still improving dust management. If you haven't already done a silica dust risk assessment for your business, you need one.

## Source Quote

> Anyone who grinds, cuts, or drills concrete will be affected. We've been in contact with the relevant departments. The industry needs more pressure — keep those letters coming.
