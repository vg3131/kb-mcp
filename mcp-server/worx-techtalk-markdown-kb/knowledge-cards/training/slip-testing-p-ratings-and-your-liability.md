# Slip Testing: P-Ratings and Your Liability

## Quick Answer

Slip resistance testing (P-ratings) has been required in Australia since the late 1990s. P1 = low slip risk (wet or dry), P5 = very high slip risk environments (e.g. fish markets). Most commercial and residential polished concrete targets P3–P4. Mechanical polish can actually improve slip resistance over burnished concrete. Know the rating of your finished floor — if someone slips, you need it documented.

## Source Quote

> In Australia it's been present since the late 1990s. It got serious and is now very relevant in a lot of areas. Know your P-rating.
