# Floor Not Holding Shine After Completion

## Quick Answer

If the shine drops within days or weeks: check concrete hardness (Mohs test), verify densifier was applied to saturation at each stage, confirm sealers were applied correctly and not contaminated with polishing residue, and check what the end user is cleaning with. Eco-friendly cleaners with citrus or acid components break down sealers rapidly.
