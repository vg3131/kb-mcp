# Sealer Application: Temperature and Humidity Rules

## Quick Answer

Totally Defend and other solvent-based impregnating sealers require: ambient and surface temperature between 15–25°C, low relative humidity (below 75–80% RH), and product temperature matching ambient. In cool climates, solvents separate from solids inside the tin — the sealer looks normal but won't penetrate. Fix: warm the tin in the sun or a bucket of warm water before opening. Never apply cold sealer. In humid conditions, the solvent carrier cannot evaporate — sealer stays wet on the surface and blooms.

## Source Quote

> Solvents separate from solids in cool climates. Put the sealer in the sun or a bucket of warm water to warm it up and reactivate the molecules before use.
