# Sealer Streaking or White Haze

## Quick Answer

White haze or streaking after applying Totally Defend is almost always one of: slab or product too cold (below 10°C), applied too thickly, excess not buffed off in time, high humidity, or contamination on the surface. Solvent-based sealers flash differently in cold and humid conditions. Reheat the product, ensure surface is above 10°C, apply in thin coats, and buff off excess within 5 minutes.

## Source Quote

> Cold weather — the solvent thickens and the product can't penetrate. It sits on top and dries as a haze. Always check product and surface temp.
