# CFQ Lubricant: Hard Bonds on Hard Floors

## Quick Answer

CFQ was originally developed for road saws. Works Plus introduced it to concrete polishing as a way to enable hard-bond diamonds on burnished or trowel-finished floors that would normally wear soft bonds too quickly. Spray via backpack sprayer in front of the machine. One set of hard segments completed 8,000+ sqm with 7mm segment remaining. Game-changing for contractors who previously walked away from burnished slabs.

## Source Quote

> Full set of hards, just cut grass. 8,000 metres. 7mm left. That's what CFQ in front of the machine does.
