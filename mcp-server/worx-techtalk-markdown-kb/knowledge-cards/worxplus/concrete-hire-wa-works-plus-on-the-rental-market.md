# Concrete Hire WA: Works Plus on the Rental Market

## Quick Answer

Concrete Hire (WA) is unique among Works Plus distributors — they rent Works Plus machines and equipment alongside selling consumables. Offering rental gives contractors access to the Worx+ system without the full capital outlay of machine purchase. This is particularly valuable for contractors scaling up, testing new equipment before buying, or taking on one-off large projects that require extra machine capacity.

## Source Quote

> You're very unique — all our other distributors don't do rental. That's a pretty tough business to be in and you guys have a great name in it.
