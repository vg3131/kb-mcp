# GK King Series: 6 Years in the Making

## Quick Answer

The GK King Series grinders were unveiled at World of Concrete 2025 in Las Vegas — the culmination of over 6 years of R&D led by Hayden McDonald. The machines were developed to address the productivity ceiling of the existing RC range. Four machines appeared on the Works Plus booth. The response was described as 'overwhelming.' Early adopters reported immediate productivity gains versus the 680 RC on their first projects.
