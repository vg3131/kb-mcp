# GRT Power Grout: Why It Fills and Hardens

## Quick Answer

The GRT Power Grouting system does more than fill surface voids. It activates dormant unreacted cement in the slab surface, contributing measurable hardness. The pre-hydration step creates capillary action that draws the grout deep into the concrete. This is why a grouted floor always outperforms one that skipped the step — even when the floor looks clean.

## Source Quote

> The GRT isn't just filling holes. The water in the grout activates dormant cement in the concrete surface. It's chemistry, not just fill.
