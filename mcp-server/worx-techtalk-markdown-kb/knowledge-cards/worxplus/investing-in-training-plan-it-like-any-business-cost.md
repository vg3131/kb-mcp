# Investing in Training: Plan It Like Any Business Cost

## Quick Answer

When building your annual business plan, education needs a line item — not an afterthought. The trajectory of growth you plan for in January requires upskilling to actually execute. A contractor who plans for two new crews but doesn't train the lead operators on those crews is planning to fail. Training investment directly correlates to productivity on site, which directly correlates to margin.

## Source Quote

> How many days did you put in your business plan for education? Because whatever growth you're planning — you need to upskill to actually execute that.
