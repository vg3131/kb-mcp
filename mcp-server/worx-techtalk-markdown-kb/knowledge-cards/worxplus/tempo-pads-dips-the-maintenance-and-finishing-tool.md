# Tempo Pads (DIPs): The Maintenance and Finishing Tool

## Quick Answer

Tempo diamond impregnated pads (DIPs) are nylon burnishing pads with diamond resin media sprayed over the surface — large surface area, flexible, heat-dispersing. They are most effective at the top end of the polishing process: final gloss enhancement after 1500 resin, post-handover maintenance buffing, and surface polish work where the floor is already tight and resins would generate too much heat.

## Source Quote

> Tempo pads — great multi-use across a broad range of solutions. Particularly valuable on the surface polish or nil-exposure finish where you've already closed the floor and you want to work with a flexible, heat-dispersing tool.
