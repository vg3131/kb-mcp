# The Marketplace View: Market Contraction is Opportunity

## Quick Answer

During market contraction cycles, Works Plus sees two types of contractors: those who panic and cut prices, and those who use the slowdown to sharpen their systems, train their teams, and position themselves for the next phase of growth. Historically, contractors who maintain price discipline and invest in quality during slow periods emerge as market leaders when construction rebounds. Works Plus exists to support both the technical and business development side of that process.

## Source Quote

> Market contraction isn't always a bad thing. At the end of a hot economy, this happens. The question is what type of operator you decide to be during it.
