# The Worx+ Process: Grouting with Totally Restore

## Quick Answer

The grouting step uses GRT double segments (120/140/160 grit) simultaneously grinding and filling. Wet the floor in front without puddles, apply Totally Restore and broom out for uniform thin coverage. Push the grinder through at a steady, slow pace while product is wet. This fills pores, activates dormant cement in the surface, and creates the foundation for densification. Do not rush the pace — the grout needs dwell time under the machine to work.

## Source Quote

> Broom out Totally Restore leaving uniform coverage without puddles. Push the grinder through at a steady slow pace while wet to grout the floor.
