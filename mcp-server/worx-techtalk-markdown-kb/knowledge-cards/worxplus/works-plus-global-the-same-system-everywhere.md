# Works Plus Global: The Same System Everywhere

## Quick Answer

The Works Plus system operates identically across Australia, New Zealand, North America, and growing international markets. Contractors in New York attend the same style seminar with the same curriculum as contractors in Melbourne. The chemistry, tooling progression, and machine approach are consistent. This is a deliberate choice — it means a contractor who trained in Australia can support an international job or share knowledge with a US-based peer.

## Source Quote

> We've had people in Australia travel interstate just to attend a seminar in another state to combine it with a business trip. Same thing happening in the US now.
