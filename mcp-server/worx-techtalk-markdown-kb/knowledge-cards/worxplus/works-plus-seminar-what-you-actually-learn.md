# Works Plus Seminar: What You Actually Learn

## Quick Answer

The Works Plus seminar covers diamond tooling fundamentals (how to make them work, not just what they are), chemical chemistry (densifiers, grouts, sealers — the why, not just the how), machine operation, and site troubleshooting. The goal is to leave with enough understanding that you can diagnose and solve problems on site without a phone call. Many contractors attend multiple times — each time they bring a different problem and leave with a different answer.

## Source Quote

> When you come to the Works Plus seminar you learn all of these things in a lot of detail — so you can work things out yourself on site. Not that we don't like hearing from you.
