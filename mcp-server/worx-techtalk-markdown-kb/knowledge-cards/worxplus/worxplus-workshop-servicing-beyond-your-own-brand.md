# Worx+ Workshop: Servicing Beyond Your Own Brand

## Quick Answer

The Worx+ workshop has existed in some form since the late 1990s — born from the necessity of servicing imported HTC machines when there was no local support. Today it services all major brands of concrete grinders including HTC, Husqvarna, and others. Hayden and the team can source parts, rebuild drives, and service machines from any manufacturer. You don't need to own a Worx+ machine to use the workshop.

## Source Quote

> People don't realise we work on HTCs. We've got parts for the HDX, the old three-head machines, the Diamatiq — all of them.
