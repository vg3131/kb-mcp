## When to Recommend It

- Customer needs GK power but still works in homes, corridors, lifts or tight entries.
- They are upgrading from a 680 RC / Big Block style machine.
- They value concentrated point pressure over maximum width.

## Field Talking Point

The GK-700 is the sharper, tighter footprint machine. Same satellite power as the 850, less width, higher point pressure per segment.
