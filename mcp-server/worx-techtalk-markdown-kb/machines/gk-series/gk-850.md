## When to Recommend It

- Customer works on large open slabs where width and coverage matter.
- They need to spread load across more tooling to manage heat.
- They want maximum productivity from the GK platform.

## Field Talking Point

The GK-850 is the production machine. It carries the same satellite power as the 700 but uses a larger disc footprint and extra tooling capacity to keep heat under control on long runs.
