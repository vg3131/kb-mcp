## Profile

Milan joined his father's spray-pave and epoxy business after a career in IT, then discovered mechanically polished concrete at a 2016 Works Plus seminar. Key breakthrough: understanding proper densification ('it's not a magic timer') and edge matching. Now runs the Works Plus NSW territory, troubleshooting on-site and running seminars. His signature tooling combo: soft 30 single rocks + double medium 30 zigzags (the 'MSY Combo') for four-headed machines.
