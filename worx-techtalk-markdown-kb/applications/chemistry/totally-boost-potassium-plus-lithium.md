# Totally Boost Potassium + Lithium

Totally Boost is a two-product densifier system — Potassium for mid-stage applications (grouting through to 200 grit) and Lithium for final-stage applications (200 grit through to completion). Formulated off 25+ years in the concrete polishing industry and integrated to work with all Worx+ honing, polishing, grout and sealer products.

## Tag

Densifier · Two-Stage

## Steps

### Step 1: Apply Potassium after grouting

After GRT grouting step, apply Totally Boost Potassium by spray to point of saturation. Keep surface wet for at least 15 minutes. On modern concrete, cure for at least 1 hour after the surface is dry. On old or compromised concrete, allow an overnight cure.

### Step 2: Apply Potassium lick coats between resin stages

Apply a lighter lick coat of Potassium prior to each consecutive honing resin (50, 100 grit). Spray application, keep the surface slightly wet while running the resin. This maximises surface hardening and colour uniformity.

### Step 3: Switch to Lithium at 200 grit stage

At the 200 grit stage, switch from Potassium to Lithium. Apply by sprayer, spread with a microfibre mop. The surface is now too closed for Potassium — Lithium's smaller molecule penetrates where Potassium cannot.

### Step 4: Apply Lithium — as soon as dry, polish

As soon as the Lithium is dry to touch, continue polishing. Unlike Potassium, Lithium does not need an extended cure window. It increases surface integrity and density resulting in better reflectivity and colour correction.

## Field Tips

- Point of saturation = surface buildup of excess product. This confirms pores are fully loaded. Without visible buildup you cannot confirm maximum penetration.
- Potassium is a mid-stage formula. Lithium is a final-stage formula. Using Potassium beyond 200 grit is wasted product — the pores are already closed.
- Both products formulated to integrate specifically with Worx+ honing and polishing tools — the chemistry and tooling work together.
