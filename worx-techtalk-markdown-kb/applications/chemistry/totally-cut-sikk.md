# Totally Cut Sikk

Cut Sikk is a grinding accelerator designed to keep diamonds cool and introduce abrasion to difficult surfaces — enabling hard bond diamonds to cut through burnished, capped or hard concrete that would normally wear soft bonds quickly. A team leader kit covers 100–200 sqm depending on material removal required.

## Tag

Grinding Accelerator · Hard Slabs

## Steps

### Step 1: Wet the floor with water first

Always wet the floor with water before applying Cut Sikk. The water provides initial cooling and helps the product distribute evenly.

### Step 2: Mix and spread

Mix the Cut Sikk product and spread it over the floor surface with a broom ahead of the machine. The active particles you can see in the mix create the abrasion — the rest is for cooling.

### Step 3: Run hard bond diamonds through product

Run the machine with hard bond segments through the Cut Sikk while the surface is still wet. The combination of water cooling and Cut Sikk abrasion particles allows hard bonds to cut burnished surfaces at walking pace. This is the same approach that completed 8,000+ sqm with one set of segments with 7mm remaining.

### Step 4: Reactivate as needed

Cut Sikk can be reactivated — simply spray water over it to reactivate the product. No waste, no re-application needed if you act quickly.

## Field Tips

- The active abrasion particles in Cut Sikk are what make hard bonds viable on difficult surfaces. Water cools. Particles cut. Together they replace the need for soft bonds.
- Can be used wet or dry — wet application provides better coverage and tool cooling.
- Originally developed for road saws. Adapted for concrete polishing applications.
