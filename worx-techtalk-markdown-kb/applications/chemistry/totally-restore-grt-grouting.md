# Totally Restore (GRT Grouting)

Totally Restore is the Worx+ grouting compound used with GRT metal bond segments to simultaneously grind and fill the pores and voids exposed by the initial metal grind. Pre-wetting the floor before grouting is critical — it changes how the product absorbs and bonds.

## Tag

Grout · GRT System

## Steps

### Step 1: Pre-wet the floor

Before applying Totally Restore, wet the floor with clean water. The pre-wet creates capillary action — it draws the grouting compound deeper into the concrete. A dry concrete surface will not absorb the product the same way. This step is non-negotiable.

### Step 2: Prepare Totally Restore

Mix Totally Restore with hot water to your desired consistency. Thicker mix = faster dry, harder cure. The product changes colour as it absorbs — watch for the colour change as your guide.

### Step 3: Apply and broom out

Apply Totally Restore to the floor and spread evenly with a broom. Uniform coverage without puddles. The GRT will work it into the floor — your job is to distribute it evenly ahead of the machine.

### Step 4: Run GRT segments while wet

Apply GRT segments to the machine and run through the product while it is still wet. The optimal time to work Totally Restore is when it turns blue — this is when it is sticky and has absorbed moisture and lined the pores. Work the machine at a steady, slow pace.

### Step 5: Reactivate if needed

If the product starts to dry too fast, spray additional water on top — this reactivates it. Do not discard or skip — reactivation is easy and free.

### Step 6: Allow to cure, then densify

After GRT grouting is complete, allow the floor to cure. Then apply Totally Boost Potassium as the first densifier step. The grouted + densified surface is now ready for resin polishing.

## Field Tips

- Pre-wet one side of a sponge with water, apply Totally Restore to both sides — the water side absorbs product instantly. The dry side repels it. The same principle applies to concrete.
- The colour change (turning blue) indicates the product has loaded the pores and is at optimal working consistency — this is the moment to run the GRT through it.
- Totally Restore also activates dormant unreacted cement in the slab surface — this is why grouted floors are consistently harder than ungrouted ones.
