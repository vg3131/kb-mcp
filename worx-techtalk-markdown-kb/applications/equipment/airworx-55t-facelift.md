# Airworx 55T (Facelift)

The Airworx 55T is the Worx+ three-phase dust extractor — the most called-about machine in the range. The facelift version is more compact (fits standard-height vans), has 15% more primary filter surface area, 19 filter socks for more effective suction, and redesigned handles that double as broom/vacuum storage. Remote start/stop, variable speed drive.

## Tag

Dust Extraction · Three Phase

## Steps

### Step 1: Load onto vehicle

Low-height profile fits standard-height vans and trailers without the clearance issues of the previous model. Fits on a standard pallet for shipping. Redesigned for easy one-person loading and unloading.

### Step 2: Connect to machine

Use appropriate hose and adaptor for your machine. Position the 55T where the hose creates minimal drag on the grinding path. Remote start/stop allows operation from the grinder without returning to the vacuum.

### Step 3: Set variable speed

Variable speed drive gives more than 40% airflow over any other dust extractor in the same power class. Start at a lower speed and increase until suction is optimal. High speed for heavy grinding, lower speed for resin work.

### Step 4: Monitor filter bags

Target a bag change every 10–12 minutes for productive grinding. Longer than 15 minutes = either the floor is very hard and not cutting, or the filter is becoming blocked. Check filter condition regularly.

### Step 5: Bag changes

New flatter vacuum cone design provides better access for bag changes. 19 filter socks provide longer clean-filter periods between full cleans. Empty bags before they are completely full to maintain suction.

## Field Tips

- A blocked filter is the #1 cause of poor suction — clean the filter socks regularly, not just when suction drops noticeably.
- 40% more airflow than competitors in the same power class. When your machine is cutting well, the vacuum must be able to keep up or productivity suffers.
- Remote start/stop is not optional — it eliminates walking back to start the vacuum and keeps the operator at the machine.
