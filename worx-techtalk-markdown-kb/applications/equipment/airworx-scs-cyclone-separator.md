# Airworx SCS (Cyclone Separator)

The Airworx SCS is a single-chamber cyclonic dust separator that sits between the grinding machine and the vacuum. It captures up to 90% of coarse particles before they reach the vacuum — keeping filters clean longer, extending vacuum operation time, and critically, enabling dust bag changes without turning off the vacuum. Increases productivity by up to 25%.

## Tag

Dust Management · Separator

## Steps

### Step 1: Position between machine and vacuum

The SCS connects in-line between the floor grinder and the 55T vacuum using the standard hose system. Height-adjustable for compact transport. Position where bag changes can be done without disrupting the grinding run.

### Step 2: Understand the bypass valve

The unique bypass valve allows continuous airflow through the separator to the vacuum during bag changes. This means the grinder can keep running while the bag is changed — unlike a direct-to-vacuum setup.

### Step 3: Monitor bag fill rate

With the SCS installed, bags fill with coarse separated material. Target 25kg every 12 minutes for productive grinding. The 3-minute bag change gives you 4 bag changes per hour. With the SCS and bypass, the machine never stops.

### Step 4: Change bags without stopping

Activate the bypass valve, change the bag, reconnect — the vacuum and machine continue operating throughout. This is the productivity gain. 1.5 extra hours of grinding in an 8-hour day versus stopping to change bags.

## Field Tips

- 90% particle separation before the vacuum = filters stay clean far longer. Reduced maintenance frequency and lower filter replacement costs.
- Up to 25% productivity increase from continuous operation. On a day rate, this is significant margin improvement with no additional cost.
- Available in continuous bag or standard dust bag configurations — choose based on project scale and waste removal setup on site.
