# Worx+ E-Sprayer

The E-Sprayer is a battery-operated mobile airless sprayer for applying water-based liquids on site. 30 litre capacity, 4-caster + 2-large-wheel chassis, 18V lithium battery (60 min continuous use, 90 min recharge), 2.6L/min delivery rate, 10m hose with click fittings. Designed for applying densifiers, grouting water, and maintenance products.

## Tag

Equipment · Application

## Steps

### Step 1: Fill with water-based product only

The E-Sprayer is for water-based products only — do not use with solvent-based sealers or any solvent-containing product. The strainer in the top prevents debris entering the unit.

### Step 2: Adjust tip for spray pattern

The adjustable tip controls spray pattern width and intensity. For densifier application, a wide fan pattern covers more area per pass. For targeted application (e.g. specific repair areas), narrow the pattern.

### Step 3: Wheel and position

Four lockable caster wheels allow the sprayer to be positioned precisely and locked in place during use. Large rear wheels enable easy site navigation. Lock castors when stationary to prevent roll.

### Step 4: Apply product ahead of machine

For densifier application, spray 5–10m ahead of the machine and allow brief penetration time before the machine runs through. The E-Sprayer delivery rate (2.6L/min) allows continuous coverage at walking pace.

### Step 5: Drain and clean after use

Drain plug at the bottom makes cleaning easy. Flush with clean water after each use. Click fittings on the tip, hose and trigger make disassembly for cleaning simple.

## Field Tips

- 30 litre capacity vs traditional pump/backpack sprayers (typically 8–16 litres) means far fewer refills on large commercial projects.
- 60 minutes continuous use per battery at maximum setting. Two batteries included — continuous swapping gives unlimited run time on large jobs.
- Drip cup on the side prevents unwanted drips reaching the floor during transport and repositioning.
