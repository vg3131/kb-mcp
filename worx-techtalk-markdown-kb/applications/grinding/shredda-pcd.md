# Shredda PCD

The Shredda PCD is a new-generation PCD tool featuring five truncated inverted PCD segments uniquely arranged for shredding medium-to-thick surface contaminants — tile adhesive, levelling compound, trowelled-down epoxy — faster than the impact PCD while leaving the substrate cleaner.

## Tag

Rapid Removal · PCD

## Steps

### Step 1: Confirm material type

Shredda is designed for medium-to-thick surface contaminants. Thin coatings are better handled with standard impact PCDs. Confirm the material before switching.

### Step 2: Load the machine

On a GK850, run 6 per disc — 18 total for a full set. The higher segment count reduces drag on mechanical components versus the impact PCD.

### Step 3: Run dry

PCDs work dry. Water is not required. The inverted segment geometry creates high point pressure for immediate penetration and shredding action.

### Step 4: Monitor substrate condition

The Shredda leaves the concrete beneath smoother and cleaner than impact PCDs which can damage the substrate. Check the floor surface regularly — stop if you see substrate damage.

### Step 5: Transition to metals

Once the contaminant layer is fully removed, transition to metal bond diamonds (30 grit ROX) to begin the grinding and polishing process.

## Field Tips

- Five truncated segments with inverted PCD geometry = immediate penetration. The extra points also reduce machine wear vs impact PCDs.
- Integrated super-hard bond diamond segment provides depth control and extends lifespan.
- Ideal on GK series machines. The large footprint and wide point distribution makes operation noticeably smoother than impact PCDs.
