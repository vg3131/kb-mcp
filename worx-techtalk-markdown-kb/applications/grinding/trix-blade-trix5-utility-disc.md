# Trix Blade & Trix5 Utility Disc

The Trix Blade is a handheld diamond blade for eliminating corner triangles and cleaning out joints and cracks before filling. Available in 30 grit and 100 grit. Used with angle grinder for precise edge and detail work.

## Tag

Edge Work · Handheld

## Steps

### Step 1: Select grit

30 grit for opening up corners, chasing cracks, and initial material removal. 100 grit for finer edge work and blending into the polished surface.

### Step 2: Chase cracks and joints

Run the Trix Blade along cracks and expansion joints to open them up and remove loose material before applying Totally Repair products. Clean V-groove profile provides better mechanical bond for repair compounds.

### Step 3: Eliminate corner triangles

Run the blade into corners at a low angle. The Trix Blade makes short work of corner triangles that hand grinders cannot reach. Work progressively from coarser to finer.

### Step 4: Combine with honeycomb and Halo tools

For full edge recovery, combine the Trix Blade with 3" honeycomb pads, 5" Halo5 pads, and corner polishing resins. This sequence matches the body machine process as closely as possible.

## Field Tips

- The Trix Blade can recover edges faster than a previous contractor ruined them — these tools are purpose-built for difficult edge scenarios.
- Always match edge work grit to body machine grit at each stage. The biggest mistake is finishing edges at a different grit to the body.
