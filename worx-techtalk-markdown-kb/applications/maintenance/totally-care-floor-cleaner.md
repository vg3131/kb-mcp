# Totally Care Floor Cleaner

Totally Care is a pH-neutral floor cleaner free from bases, phosphates and gloss agents. Formulated specifically for mechanically polished concrete — does not leave residues that attract dust or accelerate surface wear. Use in combination with regular Tempo pad buffing for a complete maintenance system.

## Tag

Maintenance · Floor Care

## Steps

### Step 1: Clear debris first

Sweep or vacuum the floor to remove dust and debris before mopping. Cleaning over loose particles will scratch the surface and drag dirt across the floor.

### Step 2: Dilute correctly

For every 10 litres of water, add 20–40ml of Totally Care. Do not use undiluted — the concentrated product is designed for this dilution ratio.

### Step 3: Mop or scrub

Apply using a mop and bucket or industrial floor scrubbing machine. Totally Care is compatible with both methods. Work in sections, overlapping each pass.

### Step 4: Allow to dry

Allow the floor to dry naturally. No rinsing required at the dilution rate specified.

### Step 5: Buff with Tempo pad

Combine regular Totally Care cleaning with periodic buffing using a Tempo pad at the same grit the floor was finished at. This is the complete maintenance system that protects the floor long-term.

## Field Tips

- The absence of gloss agents is deliberate — gloss agents in regular floor cleaners build up on polished surfaces and accelerate wear.
- Regular cleaning + Tempo buffing is not just maintenance — it is the system that keeps clients happy long after handover and generates referrals.
- Always specify Totally Care to clients at handover. Never let a client clean polished concrete with whatever they happen to have on hand.
