# Totally Repair Redi-Fix

The Redi-Fix is a one-component fast-setting repair product in a cartridge — cementitious/resin blend, ready to go with just a pull of the trigger. No mixing, no tools. Self-mixing nozzle delivers uniform material directly into the repair area. Sets in 20–30 minutes.

## Tag

Repair · One-Component · Fast

## Steps

### Step 1: Prepare the repair area

Surface must be dry, clean and free of loose material. For cracks and holes, remove any crumbling or loose concrete. For broken pieces, ensure broken faces are clean.

### Step 2: Prime the nozzle

Fit the self-mixing nozzle to the cartridge and load into the dispensing gun. Dispense a small amount onto scrap cardboard and allow material to emerge until a uniform colour is achieved — this purges any unmixed material from the nozzle.

### Step 3: Apply

For holes, saw cuts and small cracks: apply Redi-Fix directly into the repair area. For broken pieces: apply liberally to both faces to adhere back together. For deeper damage: mix in native aggregate for a more uniform result and colour match.

### Step 4: Colour matching

Mix tints with the Redi-Fix material if colour matching is required. Work within a 5–10 minute window after mixing tints before setting begins.

### Step 5: Allow to set then process

Expect 20–30 minutes to set after application. Once set, follow your normal flooring process to achieve the desired finish.

## Field Tips

- No mixing, no additional tools. This is the fastest repair method in the Worx+ range — ideal for on-site, on-the-day fixes.
- The self-mixing nozzle is the key — two components mixed at the nozzle ensures correct ratio every time without manual mixing.
- For deeper concrete damage, mixing in native aggregate improves the final visual result significantly.
