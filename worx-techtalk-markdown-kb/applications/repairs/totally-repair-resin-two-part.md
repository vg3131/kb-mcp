# Totally Repair Resin (Two-Part)

Totally Repair Resin is a two-part fast-curing resin for small cracks, spalls and holes. Key principle: always use native aggregate materials (from the actual slab) mixed with the resin for best colour match. Break all repair edges to create a natural profile and maximise mechanical adhesion.

## Tag

Repair · Resin · Fast-Set

## Steps

### Step 1: Prepare the repair area

Break in the edges of cracks or spalls to create a natural, non-straight profile — this helps the repair blend visually and creates a stronger mechanical bond. Straight, saw-cut edges look like repairs; natural edges blend.

### Step 2: Collect native aggregate

Collect small pieces of the actual concrete being repaired. Mix these with the resin (Part A) before adding the hardener (Part B). Native materials produce dramatically better colour matching than importing foreign aggregate.

### Step 3: Mix Part A + aggregate first

Combine aggregate with Part A resin and get the consistency right before adding Part B. Once Part B is added, the clock starts — 5–10 minute working window. Adding Part B too early wastes material.

### Step 4: Apply and allow to cure

Fill the repair area. At 20°C, expect 45 minutes to dry up nice and hard. Surface may be slightly sticky on top — this is normal. Do not disturb until fully cured.

### Step 5: Grind back and polish

Once set, grind the repair back flush with the surrounding floor surface. Polish through your normal resin progression to the same finish level as the surrounding floor.

## Field Tips

- Native materials vs foreign: the difference in colour match is dramatic. Always collect material from the actual slab being repaired.
- A strong mechanical adhesion property means the resin bonds aggressively — but the edge prep is still important for visual integration.
- Two-part fast-curing resin is excellent for short time-framed projects where overnight repairs are not possible.
