# GK Series

GK-700 & GK-850 · technical reference · machine selection · operation guide


## Quick Summary

GK Series at a glance: the GK-700 and GK-850 share the same 22.3 kW satellite motor and split chassis concept. The 700 concentrates that power into a narrower footprint; the 850 spreads it across a larger footprint for higher coverage and heat management.

## Platform Features

### Split Chassis

Adjust head weight while the machine is running. Start with the chassis tipped back to reduce load, then shift forward progressively as the machine stabilises.

### Dual-Motor Planetary System

The satellite motor and planetary head are driven separately, giving the operator stronger control over load, speed and cut behaviour.

### Flat Belt Drive

Flat belts were selected to handle heat and tracking loads better than the earlier poly-V prototype approach.

# GK Series Operation Guide

## Step 1: Start with soft 30 single segments

Use this as the baseline shakedown setup. Singles give maximum point pressure and quickly show how the slab is reacting.

## Step 2: Start light, then load the head

With aggressive tooling, start around positions 8–10. Once stable, progressively move toward position 0 while watching current draw.

## Step 3: Use current draw as the floor feedback

Rising amps means load is increasing. If it spikes, reduce head weight or adjust tooling. If it falls, add weight or increase production speed.

## Step 4: Use the 850’s extra slots for heat control

More segments spread the machine weight across more contact area. This is the 850’s advantage on long commercial runs.

## Step 5: Back off over ridges and soft spots

Shift the chassis back before ridges, joints or soft patches, then reload the head once the machine is stable again.

## Step 6: Recalibrate wheels after hub disconnection

Use the wheel calibration menu to align and lock each wheel correctly before operation.

# GK Series Maintenance and FAQ

## Never Pressure Wash

Use a stiff brush, scraper or compressed air. High-pressure water can force moisture into motors, electronics and bearings. Moisture in the motor housing can turn a cleaning job into a repair job.

## Dust Control Is Critical

The GK produces dust faster than a 680/820 class machine. Keep the Airworx 55T filter socks clean, bags changed and hoses clear.

## Power Requirements

22.3 kW satellite + 1.8 kW planetary is roughly 24 kW full draw. Use correctly sized three-phase power. A 30 KVA generator is the minimum practical benchmark.

## Tooling Fit

GK-700 uses 290 mm Redi-Loc discs. GK-850 uses 350 mm Redi-Loc discs. The 850 has more tool slots, so use them to spread weight and manage heat.

## Common Questions

### Can I run it on single phase?

No. The GK platform requires three-phase power.

### Which model should I recommend?

GK-700 for tighter access and sharper point pressure. GK-850 for bigger open slabs and maximum coverage.

### How does it compare to PG820?

The GK uses a 22.3 kW satellite motor and split chassis weight control. It is designed around higher down pressure and higher production output.
