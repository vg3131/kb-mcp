# Polished Concrete Process Guide

## Overview

Worx+ Mechanical Polish — Full Process Guide This is the recommended step-by-step process using Worx+ tooling and chemistry. Product links open directly in the Worx+ catalogue. Hover any step for details. Always run a test patch on a new slab before committing to the full process.

## Phase 1 — Surface Grinding

### Step 1: #30 Grit — Single Segments

Expose aggregate and establish the floor profile. Mist a little water in front of the machine to keep diamonds cool. Use Cut Sikk on burnished or hard-capped slabs where water alone isn't enough.

> 💡 Hard slab tip: If diamonds aren't cutting, apply Cut Sikk via backpack sprayer directly in front of the machine. Hard bond diamonds + Cut Sikk = no need for soft bonds on burnished surfaces.

Related products:
- [30 Grit Singles](https://www.worxplus.com/products/diamond-tools/grinding-tools)
- [Cut Sikk](https://www.worxplus.com/products/cut-sikk)

### Step 2: #50 Grit — Soft Bond

Remove 30-grit scratch pattern and refine aggregate exposure. Mist water in front of the machine to aid production rate and diamond longevity.

Related products:
- [50 Grit Softs](https://www.worxplus.com/products/diamond-tools/grinding-tools)

## Phase 2 — Grouting

### Step 3: GRT Grouting — Double Segments (120/140/160 Grit)

Wet floor in front of grinder — no puddles. Apply Totally Restore onto floor and broom out for uniform coverage. Push grinder through at a steady, slow pace while product is wet to fill all pores and voids. The GRT simultaneously grinds and works the grout into the surface.

> 💡 Process note: Keep broom movement side-to-side ahead of the machine. Puddles = product waste and poor penetration. Uniform thin coat = maximum pore fill.

Related products:
- [Totally Restore](https://www.worxplus.com/products/grout/totally-restore)
- [GRT Segments](https://www.worxplus.com/products/diamond-tools/grinding-tools)

## Phase 3 — Densification

### Step 4: Totally Boost Potassium — First Full Application

After grouting, apply Totally Boost Potassium and keep surface WET for 20–30 minutes . Re-apply in areas that dry fast. Entire area must look darker in colour when dry. Allow to cure and harden for 2–4 hours before proceeding.

> 💡 Saturation check: The floor must be consistently darker across the whole area before you move on. Any light patches = insufficient densification. Potassium is heavier — it works at this early open-pore stage. Do not rush the curing window.

Related products:
- [Totally Boost (K)](https://www.worxplus.com/products/densifier/totally-boost-potassium)

## Phase 4 — Resin Polishing

### Step 5: #50 Specialty Resin — With Potassium Assist

Spray a fine mist of Totally Boost Potassium 5–10m ahead of the machine. Commence with #50 Specialty resin while product is still wet. The wet potassium cools the tool and increases cutting speed. Ideal speed: 700–1000 RPM.

Related products:
- [#50 Specialty Resin](https://www.worxplus.com/products/diamond-tools/polishing-tools)
- [Totally Boost (K)](https://www.worxplus.com/products/densifier/totally-boost-potassium)

### Step 6: Lick Coat — Totally Boost Potassium

Apply a lick coat of potassium densifier between resin stages. Use a microfibre mop if unsure on application amount — this keeps it thin and controlled.

Related products:
- [Totally Boost (K)](https://www.worxplus.com/products/densifier/totally-boost-potassium)

### Step 7: #100 Specialty Resin — Fine Water Mist

Spray an even finer mist than with #50s to keep the tool cool. The floor surface is now denser — less water needed, finer control required.

Related products:
- [#100 Specialty Resin](https://www.worxplus.com/products/diamond-tools/polishing-tools)

### Step 8: Lick Coat — Totally Boost Potassium (Flat Microfibre)

Apply another lick coat of potassium densifier. Use a flat microfibre to control application thickness — this stage requires precision as the surface is closing.

Related products:
- [Totally Boost (K)](https://www.worxplus.com/products/densifier/totally-boost-potassium)

### Step 9: #200 Specialty Resin — Completely Dry

Run this step completely dry . The surface is now dense enough that water would hinder rather than help. No misting.

Related products:
- [#200 Specialty Resin](https://www.worxplus.com/products/diamond-tools/polishing-tools)

### Step 10: Lick Coat — Totally Boost Lithium (Microfibre)

Switch to Lithium densifier at this stage. Lithium has a smaller molecule — it penetrates closed, dense surfaces where potassium can no longer enter. Apply lick coat via microfibre.

> 💡 Why switch to Lithium: Potassium is too large to penetrate a fully densified surface. Lithium's smaller molecule finds the remaining micro-pores. Applying potassium at this stage is wasted product.

Related products:
- [Totally Boost (Li)](https://www.worxplus.com/products/densifier/totally-boost-lithium)

### Step 11: #400 Universal — Dry | Stop Here for Mid Gloss

Dry. Stop here for a mid-gloss finish. Clean the floor with a dry microfibre mop before applying sealer.

Related products:
- [#400 Universal](https://www.worxplus.com/products/diamond-tools/polishing-tools)

### Step 12: #800 → #1500 Flex → #3000 Flex — All Dry

Continue dry for high gloss. #1500 Flex and #3000 Flex for maximum clarity and reflectivity. Stop at #3000 for high gloss. Clean with dry microfibre before sealing.

Related products:
- [Flex Pads](https://www.worxplus.com/products/diamond-tools/polishing-tools)

## Phase 5 — Sealing

### Step 13: Totally Defend Impregnating Sealer — 1–2 Coats

Clean floor with dry microfibre first. Shake sealer well before opening. Do not use cold sealer — solvents separate from solids in cool climates. Warm the tin in the sun or a bucket of warm water to reactivate the molecules. Apply 1–2 very light coats via microfibre mop. Once dry (approx. 30–40 min), buff the entire floor with a white or blue pad using a burnisher or polivac.

> ⚠️ Cold weather warning: If sealer is cold when applied it will not penetrate correctly and will bloom (white haze) on the surface. Always check product temp before opening. See troubleshooting tab for sealer failure restoration methods.
