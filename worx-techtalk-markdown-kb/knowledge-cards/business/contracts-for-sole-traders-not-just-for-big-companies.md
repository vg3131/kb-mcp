# Contracts for Sole Traders: Not Just for Big Companies

## Quick Answer

Contracts and service agreements are not just for large businesses. A sole trader has significant investment in machinery, equipment, and time — all of which need protection. The smaller the business, the more important it is to document scope, payment terms, and completion conditions. A simple service agreement that sets expectations is not about distrust — it's about protecting your livelihood. Every contractor, regardless of size, should be comfortable using one.

## Source Quote

> I don't think there's ever a scenario where a service agreement isn't a great idea for a sole trader. You're protecting your livelihood — you're worthy of that protection.
