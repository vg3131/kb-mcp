# External Pressures: Turn the Negative Into a Positive

## Quick Answer

External pressures — pricing competition, staff availability, educated customers with unrealistic expectations, new tooling on the market — are constant. The operators who thrive are those who use pressure as a signal to measure, improve, and systematise. When pricing pressure rises, the answer isn't to lower your price — it's to understand your cost per sqm better than your competitors. Measure, track, and use the data to compete smarter.

## Source Quote

> External pressures — you can cut them square between the eyes or roll with the punches. Use the negative as a positive. Get some metrics and find where you're losing.
