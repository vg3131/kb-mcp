# Inflation and Pricing: Revisiting the Conversation

## Quick Answer

Inflation is a recurring challenge — the same conversation comes up annually. When input costs rise (fuel, labour, tooling, chemicals), prices must rise with them. The mistake is absorbing cost increases silently to 'stay competitive'. Over time this erodes margin to the point of unsustainability. The contractors who survive inflationary periods are those who review their base rates regularly and communicate price increases confidently.

## Source Quote

> Inflation keeps coming up — same topic, a year later. It's that time of year when everyone's talking about money because the cost of everything else has gone up.
