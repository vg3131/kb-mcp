# Knowing Your Market Sector Before You Enter

## Quick Answer

The market has multiple segments: full stone mechanical polish residential, full stone commercial, surface polish residential, grind and seal commercial, industrial grind. Each has different rates, competition levels, entry costs, and skill requirements. Choosing the wrong segment to enter — based on what others charge rather than what you can deliver — is the most common business mistake for new operators.

## Source Quote

> Assessing any market — have a plan. Know where you want to go and more importantly why you want to go there.
