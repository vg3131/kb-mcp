# Laws of Attraction: You Get What You Put Out

## Quick Answer

The type of work you attract is a direct result of the message you broadcast. If your social media, vehicle signage, and materials look budget, you attract budget clients. If they look premium, you attract clients who expect quality and will pay for it. What you present determines what comes back.

## Source Quote

> Business is like life. Whatever message you put out is what you can expect to get back.
