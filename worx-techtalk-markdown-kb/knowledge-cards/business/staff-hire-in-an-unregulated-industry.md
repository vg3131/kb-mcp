# Staff Hire in an Unregulated Industry

## Quick Answer

There's no trade qualification for concrete grinding and polishing. This creates a real challenge when hiring — how do you profile a candidate? The best operators come from related trades (tiling, flooring, construction) or are complete newcomers with the right attitude. Skills can be trained; attitude cannot. Build a skills pathway document so new starters know what they're working towards.

## Source Quote

> There's no trade, no qualification. How do we profile it? How do we get the right people in?
