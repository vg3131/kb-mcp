# The Multi-Contractor Handover Problem

## Quick Answer

If a client engages Contractor A for the first cut, Contractor B for densifying and grouting, and Contractor C for the finish — all three will blame each other for defects. Nobody owns the outcome. The result is a floor where finger-pointing prevents resolution. Educate clients: polished concrete is a single-contractor product and the best result comes from one team owning the full process.

## Source Quote

> Three contractors, one floor — who owns the defects? Everyone will point at each other. Sounds like a mess? It is.
