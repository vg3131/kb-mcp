# The Sky Is Falling: How to Think During Market Uncertainty

## Quick Answer

When construction approval rates drop and phones go quieter, two things happen: contractors who built their business during a boom panic, and contractors who've built on systems and quality look for opportunities. Guys selling full setups at fire-sale prices means an opportunity to buy quality second-hand equipment. A quieter phone is an opportunity to train, systematise, and market. Cautious optimism — not denial, not panic — is the correct position.

## Source Quote

> The sky is falling, take cover. Am I joking? Yes. But the question is what type of operator you decide to be when it gets rough.
