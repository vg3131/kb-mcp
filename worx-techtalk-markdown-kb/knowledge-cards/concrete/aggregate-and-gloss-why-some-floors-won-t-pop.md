# Aggregate and Gloss: Why Some Floors Won't Pop

## Quick Answer

The combination of aggregate type, mix design, and the polishing process determines whether a floor achieves a crisp, high-clarity result. Concrete with a high proportion of manufactured sand, recycled aggregate, or dark/grey basalt aggregate can look dull at 400+ grit even with perfect technique. The aggregate itself doesn't polish to the same reflectivity as quartz or crystolic stone. Set expectations at quoting stage — and run a test patch to confirm achievable gloss.

## Source Quote

> At the 400 grit stage it was lacking that crispness. As we got higher up the aggregate just wasn't giving us what we wanted. The material doesn't always cooperate.
