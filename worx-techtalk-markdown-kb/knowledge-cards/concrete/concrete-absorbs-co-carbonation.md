# Concrete Absorbs CO₂ (Carbonation)

## Quick Answer

Concrete structures absorb carbon dioxide over their lifetime. Internal floors carbonate faster than external (no rain washing it away). This absorption isn't currently being calculated in green building assessments — a missed opportunity.

## Source Quote

> Build more concrete, plant trees next door. Concrete isn't necessarily evil — it's the most used building material.
