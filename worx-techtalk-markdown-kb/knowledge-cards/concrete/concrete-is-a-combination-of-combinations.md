# Concrete is a Combination of Combinations

## Quick Answer

Concrete is defined by a word meaning 'to grow together' — it's a combination of different materials. The polishing process is also a combination of different materials. Understanding how these interact (aggregate, cement, admixtures, fibres, supplementary cementitious materials, curing compounds) is what separates polishers who problem-solve from those who just follow a step chart. Every variable in the mix affects how you process the surface.

## Source Quote

> Concrete comes from a word meaning 'to grow together'. Our process is also a combination of different materials — combination with combination, layers upon layers.
