# Edges Tell the Whole Story

## Quick Answer

Hand-trowelled edges show lighter colour, chatter marks, and pushed-down aggregate. If edges look bad, every mid-floor defect gets scrutinised. Get the edges right first — the rest becomes forgivable.

## Source Quote

> If the edges are bad, every defect gets looked at. If the edges are good, no one looks in the middle.
