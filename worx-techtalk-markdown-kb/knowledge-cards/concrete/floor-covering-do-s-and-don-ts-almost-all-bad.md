# Floor Covering Do's and Don'ts: Almost All Bad

## Quick Answer

Covering a finished or in-progress polished floor is almost always problematic. Plastic causes moisture variation and colour changes. MDF with plastic traps moisture. Ramboard (hard board) is better but still restricts vapour. The safest option for short-term protection is calico (painter's drop sheets). For longer periods, leave the floor uncovered and communicate with the builder about foot traffic and trade access.
