# Fly Ash & Slag Float to the Surface

## Quick Answer

These lighter SCMs concentrate at the top — right where you're trowelling and polishing. Above 30% replacement, surface hardness drops. Apply densifier immediately after finishing to offset. The green movement is pushing replacement higher each year.

## Source Quote

> Above 30% slag, there may be hardness issues at the surface.
