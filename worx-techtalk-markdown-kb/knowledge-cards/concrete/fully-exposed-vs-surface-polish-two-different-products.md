# Fully Exposed vs Surface Polish: Two Different Products

## Quick Answer

Tim Van der Veen explains: fully exposed (aggregate visible) vs surface polish (salt & pepper or burnish) are mechanically and chemically different products. Australia leads globally in deep-cut fully exposed work. The northern hemisphere predominantly does surface polish. Understanding which product you're selling changes everything about your process, pricing and client expectation.
