# Manufactured Sands Pop Out Late

## Quick Answer

Natural sands (spherical, smooth) pop out during early grinding. Crusher dust (wedge-shaped, porous) bonds more strongly and doesn't release until polishing stages (100–400 grit) — causing scratches and aggregate pop-outs. Keep crusher dust below 30% of sand fraction.

## Source Quote

> You're working harder to get crusher dust out — hence seeing it in the later stages.
