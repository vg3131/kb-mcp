# Topping Slabs: The Better Choice for Polish

## Quick Answer

80–100mm thick, no attached beams, poured after walls are up. Only 3–4 truck loads, controlled environment, can align joints with re-entering corners. Hydronic heating makes the upsell easy. Far more predictable than polishing a structural raft slab.

## Source Quote

> Topping slabs are a lot more reliable. If they don't work, it's not a bomb to replace part of it.
