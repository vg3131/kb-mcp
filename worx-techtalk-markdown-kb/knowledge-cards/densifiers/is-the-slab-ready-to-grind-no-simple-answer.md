# Is the Slab Ready to Grind? No Simple Answer

## Quick Answer

Time-based rules (e.g. 'grind after 28 days') ignore the real variables: environment (inside vs outside), temperature, slump, whether it's on-ground or suspended, and slab thickness. The best test is physical: scratch test with a nail for hardness, and check moisture with plastic sheeting taped for 24 hours. If moisture beads under the sheet, it's too wet for a sealer — but may still be grindable.

## Source Quote

> Is it ready to grind after five days? I don't know. It depends on so many factors — environment, inside or out, hot day, cold day, slump, on ground.
