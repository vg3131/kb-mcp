# Keep the Floor Wet: 10–15 Minutes First Coat

## Quick Answer

After the first application, keep the surface wet for 10–15 minutes. Water carriers help draw silicate in via capillary action. If it flashes off, surface pores seal and subsequent coats can't penetrate.

## Source Quote

> Get it down heavy first and keep it wet for 10–15 minutes — that's the concrete getting the opportunity to draw the product in.
