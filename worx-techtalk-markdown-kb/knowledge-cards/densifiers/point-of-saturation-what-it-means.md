# Point of Saturation: What It Means

## Quick Answer

You've reached saturation when excess densifier builds up on the surface. Without this buildup, you can't confirm the concrete has received maximum silicate. The concrete's Part B (free lime) reacts with the densifier's Part A (silicate) to form calcium silicate hydrate — a hard crystalline structure.

## Source Quote

> Unless you're getting buildup on the surface, you don't know you've got enough in.
