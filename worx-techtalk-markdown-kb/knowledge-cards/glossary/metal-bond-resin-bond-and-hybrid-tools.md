# Metal Bond, Resin Bond and Hybrid Tools

## Quick Answer

Metal bond diamonds are sintered — diamond segments suspended in a metal matrix. Used for aggressive cutting, flattening and early-stage grinding. Resin bond diamonds use a polymer matrix — finer cut, higher clarity, used for honing and polishing. Hybrid tools combine both matrices. Bond hardness (soft to super-hard) is matched to the concrete hardness inversely — hard concrete needs a soft bond to expose fresh diamond.

## Source Quote

> Soft concrete needs a hard bond, hard concrete needs a soft bond. Getting that wrong is the single biggest tool-selection mistake.
