# Redi-Loc System: What It Means

## Quick Answer

Redi-Loc is the Worx+ quick-change diamond mounting system. Diamonds snap onto the Redi-Loc holder disc with a twist-lock mechanism — no screws, no bolts, no tools. Changing a full set of 9 diamonds takes under 2 minutes. The system also ensures each diamond is seated at the correct angle and height. All Worx+ metal bond grinding tools use Redi-Loc.

## Source Quote

> Redi-Loc — it's a twist-lock system. You don't need tools. Segment change takes a couple of minutes.
