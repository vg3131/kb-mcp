# Mentone Premix: Garden Supply to Decorative Pioneer

## Quick Answer

Gary Finemore's family: garden supply in 1972, pivoted to exposed aggregate in 1984 after one customer asked for pebbles in the mix. Within 5 years, garden supply was gone. A business manager walked in off the street to help systematise. Then Harry brought polished concrete — and Mentone couldn't sell grey cement for months because white was in such demand.
