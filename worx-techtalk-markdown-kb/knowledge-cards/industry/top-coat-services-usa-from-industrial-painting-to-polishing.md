# Top Coat Services (USA): From Industrial Painting to Polishing

## Quick Answer

Henry and Joe Hunt at Top Coat Services (Massachusetts, USA) represent the Works Plus / Eno Supply US contractor base. Their father started in industrial painting and sandblasting, then moved into residential epoxy flooring to fill winter downtime gaps.
