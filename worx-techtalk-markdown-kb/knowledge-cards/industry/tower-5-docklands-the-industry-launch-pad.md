# Tower 5 (Docklands): The Industry Launch Pad

## Quick Answer

With Designer Crete abandoned, Harry worked with technologist David Newman and Mentone Premix on a special white concrete topping using crystolic aggregate from South Australia. Tower 5 became the first true polished concrete project using Hyper Floor — later used by Husqvarna worldwide to market 'polished concrete possibilities.'

## Source Quote

> That photo ran globally. Tower 5 was used as the example of polished concrete possibilities worldwide.
