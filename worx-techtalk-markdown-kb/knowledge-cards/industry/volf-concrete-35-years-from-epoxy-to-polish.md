# Volf Concrete: 35 Years from Epoxy to Polish

## Quick Answer

Joe from Volf Concrete Coatings and Polishing (Traralgon, VIC) has been in the industry over 35 years — originally in epoxy coatings before transitioning to polished concrete roughly 10 years ago. The pivot from coatings to polishing mirrors a broader industry shift as clients moved away from topical coatings toward mechanically polished concrete. Volf's social media presence now makes their work recognisable across the Australian polishing community.

> 35 years — mainly epoxy coatings right up until about 10 years ago, then we developed more into the polishing side and changed the company structure.
