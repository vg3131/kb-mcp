# Works Plus: The Original Worx+ TechTalks (2021)

## Quick Answer

The TechTalk series started as informal Friday afternoon sessions with Josh, Mike, Daniel and Kurt — covering hot topics that came in from the market each week. The format was deliberately casual and community-driven: viewers submitted questions, the team answered them live. Early topics included speed settings, grouting, contracts, tooling direction, and mix design. This format — practitioners answering real practitioner questions — is what built the TechTalk community.

## Source Quote

> The whole idea: if we can answer the questions we keep getting asked, hopefully we can remove the roadblock and help you move forward in what you're doing every day.
