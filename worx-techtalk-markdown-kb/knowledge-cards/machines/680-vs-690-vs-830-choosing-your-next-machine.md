# 680 vs 690 vs 830: Choosing Your Next Machine

## Quick Answer

The 680 RC is proven, versatile and the benchmark — everything works around it. The 690 has the same horsepower as the 680 but more weight and larger discs — it's underpowered for what it's carrying on Australian 415V. The 830 has genuinely more horsepower and new-generation electronic drives — it's a real step up. If your 680 RC is doing what you need, don't upgrade for the sake of it. If you're hitting a ceiling on productivity, the 830 is the move — not the 690.
