# Bush Hammers: 40-Year-Old Tech That Still Earns Its Place

## Quick Answer

Bush hammers (and scarifiers) have been used for surface preparation for 30–40 years. They're best for removing coatings, creating profile on smooth surfaces for adhesion, and opening up burnished concrete that can't be cut with metals alone. Not a grinding tool — a prep tool. Overuse chews up aggregate and creates an uneven profile that's hard to flatten.

## Source Quote

> Bush hammers are a prep tool not a polishing tool. Know what job they're doing before you decide they're the answer.
