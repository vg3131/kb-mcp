# Dedicated Equipment and Plates: Why It Matters

## Quick Answer

Keep dedicated grouting discs separate from polishing discs — grout residue bonds to metals and contaminates resin results. Keep resins on their own dedicated plates from 120 grit up. Using a ring or zip tie system to keep tools on specific discs means no bedding-in time, consistent profile, and faster results on repeat jobs. Having dedicated plates for each stage is not just about quality — it saves time on every single project.

## Source Quote

> Dedicated tooling: grouting discs separate, resins on their own plates. The continuity it gives you — faster setup, consistent profile — it pays for itself on the first job.
