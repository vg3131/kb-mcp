# Demo Head vs Spring Head: Flex Facts

## Quick Answer

The 'demo head' name is misleading — it does not mean demolition only. Demo heads actually offer MORE flex than spring heads despite spring steel's reputation. Spring heads are more limited in their movement range. Tim Van der Veen (who invented the spring head) officially apologised in Ep 32 and recommended everyone upgrade to demo heads.

## Source Quote

> I can't do this anymore talking about spring heads being better for polishing. Demo heads have more flex — non-negotiable.
