# DF Pulse Error on RC Machines: What It Means

## Quick Answer

The DF pulse fault is one of the most common errors on RC machines. When powered up and the remote switch is flipped, nothing happens — drives drop out. Root cause is usually a faulty speed sensor. Fix: check the sensor signal wire first before assuming a full drive failure. A $40 sensor can save a $400+ service call.

## Source Quote

> The DF pulse — drives drop out and nothing happens. It's a tricky one because a lot of people don't even know this exists on their machine.
