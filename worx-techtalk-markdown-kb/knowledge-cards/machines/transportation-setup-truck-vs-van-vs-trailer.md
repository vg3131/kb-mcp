# Transportation Setup: Truck vs Van vs Trailer

## Quick Answer

No single setup suits everyone — it depends on job type, machine size, and whether you're running single phase or three phase. Common setups: van + trailer for generator and machine, truck with a tray for full kit, or purpose-built enclosed trailer. Whatever you choose, build the setup so you can unload solo in under 10 minutes. Efficiency at the start and end of the day matters as much as productivity on the floor.

## Source Quote

> One guy will preach his setup is the best in the world. Another will swear by a van and towing a generator. It's personal — but efficiency loading and unloading is non-negotiable.
