# When to Upgrade Your Machine

## Quick Answer

Before upgrading, ask: what is the upgrade supposed to achieve, how does it benefit the business, and what will it equate to in productivity? If you have 4.5 days of work per week and an upgrade means you complete it in 3 days — you've either got 1.5 days to fill or you've just improved margin. Upgrades are only valuable if the pipeline justifies them.

## Source Quote

> What's the intention? What is it supposed to do? How is it supposed to benefit the business? If you've only got three days of work a week, an upgrade just means you finish sooner.
