# 60 Grit on Edges: An American Observation

## Quick Answer

At a Works Plus/Eno Supply seminar in New York, attendees were observed running 60 grit on hand grinders along edges — a common practice in the US that Australian contractors moved away from years ago. Skipping 60s on edges (going directly from coarser grits to 100/120) saves significant time without sacrificing outcome. The floor tells you whether 60s are necessary.

## Source Quote

> I saw a guy running a 60 grit hand grinder along the edge — nearly finished, a metre from done. That's a lot of unnecessary time on your knees.
