# Black Scratches: No Water in Front of the Machine

## Quick Answer

Black scratches or 'half moons' during grouting or 120 grit stage are caused by insufficient moisture in front of the machine. Without water, the tool generates excessive heat creating thermal marks that embed in the surface. Three different contractors called Milan in one week with this issue — same root cause every time.

## Source Quote

> There wasn't any water in front of the machine — that became the big issue.
