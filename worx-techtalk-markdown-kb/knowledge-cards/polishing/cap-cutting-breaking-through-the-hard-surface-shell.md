# Cap Cutting: Breaking Through the Hard Surface Shell

## Quick Answer

Hard-capped concrete (burnished or power-trowelled to a sealed surface) is common everywhere, not just Australia. The cap prevents diamond penetration. Approach: start with softer bonds to generate more heat and open the surface, or use a CFQ-style lubricant to help hard bonds cut through. Multi-head machines can also run a bush hammer pass to break the cap before grinding commences.
