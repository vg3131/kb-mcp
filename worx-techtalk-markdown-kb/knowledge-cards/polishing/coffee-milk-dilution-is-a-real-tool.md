# Coffee & Milk: Dilution is a Real Tool

## Quick Answer

Different concentrations of the same chemical produce different results. Grouting compound diluted heavily is useful for lick coats. Densifier at full strength penetrates less deeply but reacts faster at the surface. The 'coffee and milk' analogy: everyone has a different preference for how strong their mixture is — and on different floors, different concentrations are correct.

## Source Quote

> We all have a different preference for our coffee. Almond, oat, full cream — same product, different outcomes. Same applies to how you use your chemicals.
