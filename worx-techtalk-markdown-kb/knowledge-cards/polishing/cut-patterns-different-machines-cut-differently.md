# Cut Patterns: Different Machines Cut Differently

## Quick Answer

Different machines leave visibly different scratch patterns — even at the same grit. Planetary (HTC-style) machines leave circular patterns; the Worx+ RC system leaves a different profile due to counter-rotating dual drive. Know your machine's cut pattern and use it to your advantage. A planetary cut at 30 grit may need more passes to eliminate directional marks than an RC machine at the same stage.

> Good cuts, bad cuts — are there bad cuts or just different ones we need to understand? The answer is both.
