# Double Grouting: When and Why

## Quick Answer

Double grouting means running the GRT grouting step twice before progressing to resins. Reasons to do it: high-porosity or boney concrete that absorbs the first coat, slabs where the first grout pass revealed new voids after drying, or projects where defect tolerance is extremely low. Time investment is worth it — voids at 50 grit stage are nearly impossible to recover from.

## Source Quote

> Why double grout? Because porous concrete absorbs the first coat and you're not starting with a full surface. The second coat fills what the first exposed.
