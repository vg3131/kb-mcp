# Dry vs Wet Surface Polishing: Four Different Scenarios

## Quick Answer

At a Works Plus seminar in WA, four different surface polish scenarios were tested: dry mechanical polish, wet polish, burnish finish, and combination approaches. Key finding: dry polishing gives more control over reflection and heat management at higher grits but requires the floor to be properly closed first. Wet polishing at coarser stages helps remove densifier residue and controls heat. Match the method to the concrete.

## Source Quote

> Four different surface polishes — wet, dry, burnish, combination. What the floor tells you at 200 grit determines which direction you go.
