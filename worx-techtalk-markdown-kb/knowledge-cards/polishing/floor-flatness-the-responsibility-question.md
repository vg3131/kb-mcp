# Floor Flatness: The Responsibility Question

## Quick Answer

Concrete isn't always flat — and it's not always the polisher's problem to fix. The question of who is responsible for floor flatness (builder, concreter, polisher) needs to be resolved before quoting. If a floor is out of level, grinding it flat takes significantly more time and diamond consumption. Always quote for flatness separately, document floor condition before starting, and charge accordingly. A flat floor makes everything downstream faster and better.

## Source Quote

> How flat should concrete be before I start? What obligation does the builder have? How much should I charge for flattening? These are all part of the same conversation.
