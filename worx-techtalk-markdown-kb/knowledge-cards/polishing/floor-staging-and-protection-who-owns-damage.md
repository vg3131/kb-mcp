# Floor Staging and Protection: Who Owns Damage

## Quick Answer

Staging of a polished concrete project needs to be defined before work starts. When does the contractor's responsibility end? What happens if other trades damage the floor between first grind and final polish? These questions need to be answered in the contract. The best approach: define each stage in writing, get sign-off at each stage, document the floor condition, and specify what protection (if any) the builder is responsible for.

## Source Quote

> 80% of phone calls trace back to unclear expectations. Define who is responsible at each stage — in writing. Who covers damage from other trades?
