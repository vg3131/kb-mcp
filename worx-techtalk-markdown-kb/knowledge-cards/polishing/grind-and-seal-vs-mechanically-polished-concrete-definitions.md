# Grind and Seal vs Mechanically Polished Concrete: Definitions

## Quick Answer

Grind and seal (also called cut and seal): concrete is ground to a specified profile, then a topical sealer or coating is applied. The shine comes from the coating, not the concrete. Mechanically polished concrete: diamonds progressively refine the surface — the shine comes from the polished concrete itself with an impregnating sealer that doesn't change the look. These are mechanically and chemically different products. They compete on aesthetics but serve different markets and have different maintenance requirements.

## Source Quote

> Lingo in the industry is pretty important. Grind and seal, polished concrete, mechanically polished concrete — these are genuinely different things. Know what you're making.
