# Moisture and Sealers: Never Apply in Humid Conditions

## Quick Answer

High humidity — even indoors — causes water-based and solvent-based sealers to fail. The moisture in the air prevents evaporation of the carrier, trapping it under the film and causing whitening, fish-eyes, or adhesion failure. Pool surrounds and outdoor areas are especially prone. Check relative humidity before applying any sealer. Above 85% RH, stop work.
