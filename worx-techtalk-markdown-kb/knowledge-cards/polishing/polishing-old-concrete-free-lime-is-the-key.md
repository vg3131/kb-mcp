# Polishing Old Concrete: Free Lime is the Key

## Quick Answer

Old concrete can absolutely be polished — if it still has available calcium hydroxide (free lime) to react with densifier. Free lime is the by-product of the cement hydration process. Very old concrete that has fully carbonated (absorbed all available CO₂) may have little free lime left at the surface. Test before quoting: apply a small amount of densifier and watch for reaction. No darkening = limited free lime = limited densification response.

## Source Quote

> For polishing concrete what we need is calcium hydroxide — the byproduct of water and cement hydrating. Old concrete still has it — you just need to work with what's there.
