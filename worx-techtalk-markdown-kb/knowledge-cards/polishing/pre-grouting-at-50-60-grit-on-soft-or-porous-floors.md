# Pre-Grouting at 50–60 Grit on Soft or Porous Floors

## Quick Answer

On very soft or porous concrete that is crumbling or has large voids, a pre-grouting technique at the 50–60 metal bond stage (before the GRT grouting step) packs the larger holes with fine concrete paste and stabilises the surface. This creates a better foundation for the formal GRT grouting step and prevents the resin stage from pulling aggregate. It's an additional step that soft concrete sometimes needs before the standard process begins.

## Source Quote

> On soft concrete that's falling apart, we can pre-grout using the 50-60 metal bond diamond — that fills up your larger holes and packs the floor with a bit of goodness.
