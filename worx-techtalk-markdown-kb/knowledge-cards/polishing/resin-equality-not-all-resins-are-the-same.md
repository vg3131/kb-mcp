# Resin Equality: Not All Resins Are the Same

## Quick Answer

Within the Worx+ range and across brands, resins at the same grit are not created equal. Variables include bond hardness, diamond concentration, segment geometry, and the resin binder itself. The Tempo XD resin was developed to close the gap — a harder matrix that suits a broader range of concrete hardnesses, requiring less tool selection decision-making on site.

## Source Quote

> Not all resins are created equal — whether it's our range or other brands. At the same grit you can get very different results.
