# Satin Floors: Method Matters More Than Grit

## Quick Answer

Satin finishes are increasingly demanded by architects and homeowners but are misunderstood. You can arrive at satin through a surface polish (stopping before full exposure), a burnish finish, or a hybrid approach. The challenge: a satin floor shows inconsistencies more than a high gloss or deep grind. Any variation in densification, profile, or grout fill will be clearly visible.

## Source Quote

> Gone are the days where deep grind exposed aggregate high gloss was the only product. Satin is exciting — but it shows everything.
