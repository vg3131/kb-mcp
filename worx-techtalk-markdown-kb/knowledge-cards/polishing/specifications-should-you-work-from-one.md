# Specifications: Should You Work From One?

## Quick Answer

A floor specification is a manufacturer's documented process for a flooring system — passed to architects, designers and builders. Contractors can be certified to deliver it. Pros: you get specified by architects, recurring commercial work, premium positioning. Cons: you're constrained to that spec even when site conditions require a different approach, and you're building the brand of the specifier. The debate: is your own documented process (your brand) more valuable long-term?

## Source Quote

> A specification — a contractor mines their own business, gets a call from a builder saying here's the specification to deliver. Is that better or worse than your own process being the specification?
