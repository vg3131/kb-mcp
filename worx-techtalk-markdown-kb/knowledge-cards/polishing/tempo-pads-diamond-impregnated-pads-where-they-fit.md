# Tempo Pads (Diamond Impregnated Pads): Where They Fit

## Quick Answer

Tempo pads are nylon burnishing pads with diamond resin media sprayed over the surface (diamond impregnated pads — DIPs). They're versatile: final gloss maintenance on a finished floor, light honing on surface polishes, or handover buff preparation. They don't replace resins but sit at the top of the process for gloss enhancement and maintenance. Also excellent for post-handover maintenance programs.

## Source Quote

> Tempo pads — multi-use across a broad range of flooring solutions. Really great on the top end of the process for gloss and maintenance.
