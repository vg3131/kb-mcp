# Tiger Stripes: Always Trace Backwards

## Quick Answer

Tiger stripes at 50 resin nearly always trace to uneven densifier, unflat profile, or tools not bedded on dedicated plates. The metal bond scratch lodges densifier and resists removal. Diagnose backwards — don't throw tools at the symptom.

## Source Quote

> The tiger strip is created from the last metal bond diamond — densifier lodged in there making it hard to remove.
