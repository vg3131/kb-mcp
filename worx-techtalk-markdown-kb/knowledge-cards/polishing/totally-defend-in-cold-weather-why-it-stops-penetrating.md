# Totally Defend in Cold Weather: Why It Stops Penetrating

## Quick Answer

Totally Defend is a solvent-based impregnating sealer. In cold weather, the solvent viscosity increases — the product becomes thicker and won't penetrate the surface as designed. It sits on top, dries, and creates a white haze. Fix: warm the product to room temperature before applying and ensure the slab surface temperature is above 10°C. Never apply in conditions below 5°C.

## Source Quote

> Cold weather — the solvent thickens. It's not applying like it normally does. The product is reacting to the conditions, not failing.
