# Calcium Carbonate Prevention

## Quick Answer

Water spilled on polished floors must be cleaned before it evaporates. Efflorescence and calcium carbonate rings are management issues, not product failures. Document this for building owners.

## Source Quote

> If water spills on the floor, clean it off — don't let it dry naturally.
