# Positioning Polished Concrete vs Alternatives

## Quick Answer

Your competition is not other polished concrete contractors — it's timber flooring at $80–$120/sqm, stone at $200+/sqm, and luxury vinyl at $50–$80/sqm. Polished concrete is a manufactured-on-site permanent product that can't be replaced with a 30-minute click-and-lock install. Position it on longevity, uniqueness, and the story of craftsmanship. A polished concrete floor outlasts the building it's in.

## Source Quote

> We're manufacturing a boutique product on site. No one else is doing what we're doing to that specific slab on that specific day.
