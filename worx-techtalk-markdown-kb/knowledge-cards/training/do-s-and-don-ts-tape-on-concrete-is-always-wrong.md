# Do's and Don'ts: Tape on Concrete is Always Wrong

## Quick Answer

Never put adhesive tape directly on polished or in-progress concrete. All tapes contain solvents in the adhesive. On fresh concrete (under 6 months), leaving tape down for weeks or months can leave a permanent stain from the adhesive solvent reaction. Even '14-day' tape causes problems. Document this in your contract and communicate to builders and site managers explicitly.

## Source Quote

> The only tape I approve on concrete is VHS. Outside of that — any tape, including 14-day — has chemicals that react with concrete.
