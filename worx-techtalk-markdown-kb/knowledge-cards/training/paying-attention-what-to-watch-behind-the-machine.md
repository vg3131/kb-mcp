# Paying Attention: What to Watch Behind the Machine

## Quick Answer

Experienced operators constantly monitor what's happening behind the machine — not just the path ahead. What does the scratch pattern look like? Is the densifier being worked in evenly? Are the segment marks consistent? Are there any black or tiger-striped areas developing? Good operators catch problems within one pass. Poor operators discover them at the 200 grit stage when it's too late.

## Source Quote

> Paying attention — what's happening behind the machine is your best indicator of whether everything is working as it should.
