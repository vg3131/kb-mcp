# Record Everything on the Job

## Quick Answer

As concrete mixes change, last year's process may not work. Keep a journal. Photograph every stage. Without documentation, you have nothing to learn from when things go wrong.

## Source Quote

> Make a record. Write your thoughts down. Things are not going to be the same.
