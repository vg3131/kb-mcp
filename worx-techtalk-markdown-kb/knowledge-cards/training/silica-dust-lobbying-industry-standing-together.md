# Silica Dust Lobbying: Industry Standing Together

## Quick Answer

The silica dust regulatory changes proposed from Canberra required coordinated industry response. Works Plus worked with industry bodies and encouraged all contractors to submit individual letters to the relevant departments. The most important message: don't assume someone else will do it for you. Every voice counts. The industry's ability to shape workable regulation — rather than having impractical restrictions imposed — depends on participation from every operator.

## Source Quote

> Don't think someone else is going to do it for you. Get your voice heard — this is not over yet. The more pressure, the better outcome for the whole industry.
