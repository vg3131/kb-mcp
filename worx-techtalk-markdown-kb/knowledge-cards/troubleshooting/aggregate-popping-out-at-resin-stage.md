# Aggregate Popping Out at Resin Stage

## Quick Answer

Late-stage aggregate pop-out during resin work (100–400 grit) is almost always manufactured sand (crusher dust) releasing after the matrix bond is broken down by resin heat. Crusher dust particles are wedge-shaped and bond strongly to cement paste — they survive metal bonds but release under resin friction. If this is happening, reduce RPM and run wetter at the 100–200 grit stage.

## Source Quote

> Crusher dust — it's more porous, more surface bonding. You're working harder to get it out at the later stages.
