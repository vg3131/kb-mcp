# Sealer Failure Method 1: Solvent-Assisted Surface Breakdown

## Quick Answer

For fresh or minor blooming: (1) Ventilate area, ensure 15–25°C, address any moisture. (2) Test a small inconspicuous patch first. (3) Dry mop surface. (4) Apply a light even coat of the same solvent-based sealer via pressure sprayer — the solvents in the fresh sealer reactivate and soften the failed layer. Allow 5–10 minutes dwell time, mop side-to-side repeatedly. (5) After 30 minutes, remove excess using a planetary machine with XD Burnishing Pad or HD Polishing Pad at one grit higher than the original finish. (6) Dry for 1 hour, inspect. If clean — apply final light coat per manufacturer instructions. If not — proceed to Method 2.

## Source Quote

> Apply fresh sealer to reactivate the failed layer — the solvents soften and re-emulsify the bloom. One grit higher than the original finish removes the residue.
