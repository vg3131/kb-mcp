# Sealer Failure Method 2: Full Mechanical Reinstatement

## Quick Answer

For persistent blooming where Method 1 fails: (1) Ventilate, 15–25°C, fix moisture. (2) Test 3–10 sqm first. (3) Dry mop, then apply Boost Potassium via E-Sprayer. Hone with 50-grit Specialty Pro or Impact while surface is damp. (4) Apply wet coat of Boost Potassium, mist ahead of grinder. Hone with 100-grit Universal or Impact while damp. (5) Apply Boost Lithium — E-Sprayer, even coverage, no microfibre at this stage. Allow to dry fully. (6) Mist Boost Lithium ahead of machine, hone with 200-grit Universal or Impact while damp. (7) Apply second coat Boost Lithium (microfibre OK here), dry fully. (8) Use 400-grit Flex or Tempo XD 400 dry until 100% surface refinement. Finish to satin (200 Tempo HD pads) or gloss (1500 Flex/XD 1500) then reseal.

## Source Quote

> It is imperative that all uncured sealer deposits are fully removed to restore breathability before reinstating the impregnating sealer.
