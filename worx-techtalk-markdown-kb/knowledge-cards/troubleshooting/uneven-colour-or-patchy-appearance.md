# Uneven Colour or Patchy Appearance

## Quick Answer

Patchy colour after polishing almost always traces back to: inconsistent concrete pour or cold joints, variable densifier application, floor covering after grinding (moisture variation), or inconsistent trowel finish. Diagnose which cause is present before attempting to rectify. Additional grinding rarely fixes colour issues caused by the concrete pour itself.

## Source Quote

> If the edges are bad every defect gets scrutinised. Colour variation is almost always in the concrete, not the process.
