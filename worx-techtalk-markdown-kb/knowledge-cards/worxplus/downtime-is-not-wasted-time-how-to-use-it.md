# Downtime Is Not Wasted Time: How to Use It

## Quick Answer

When a job is cancelled or postponed, the instinct is to panic. The better use of downtime: run in-house technical training with your crew, lab test new tooling combinations you've been curious about, update your safety documentation and SWMS, audit your van and replenish consumables, or reach out to clients you haven't quoted for recently. Contractors who use downtime well come out of slow periods stronger than they went in.

## Source Quote

> Downtime — it's not cancelled, it's moved forward. In-house training, lab work, safety reports, catching up online. That's not wasted time, that's investment.
