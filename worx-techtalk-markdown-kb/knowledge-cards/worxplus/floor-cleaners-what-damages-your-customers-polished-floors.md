# Floor Cleaners: What Damages Your Customers' Polished Floors

## Quick Answer

The two biggest enemies of a polished concrete floor post-handover are: tape (any adhesive tape — even 14-day tape contains solvents that react with concrete) and the wrong cleaners. So-called 'eco-friendly' or 'environmentally friendly' cleaning products often contain acids or alkaline compounds that break down the sealer and etch the concrete surface. Provide every client with a maintenance guide and a recommended cleaner.

## Source Quote

> I've seen too many floors broken down in fairly short periods of time because the end user believed concrete is bulletproof and cleaned it with whatever they liked.
