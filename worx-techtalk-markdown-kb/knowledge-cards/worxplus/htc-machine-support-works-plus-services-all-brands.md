# HTC Machine Support: Works Plus Services All Brands

## Quick Answer

Works Plus stocks accessories, heads, and service parts for HTC machines including the 650 HDX and older three-head models. Hayden and the workshop team service and rebuild HTC machines across Australia through the distributor network. Contractors don't need to own a Works Plus machine to get Works Plus workshop support. This positions Works Plus as the technical support partner for the entire industry, not just its own product ecosystem.

## Source Quote

> People don't realise we work on HTCs. We stock demo heads, drive parts, accessories — for the HDX, the Diamatiq, all of them. Any local distributor can handle it.
