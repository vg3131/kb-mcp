# Hyper Floor Densifier: The Original

## Quick Answer

Hyper Floor was the world's first commercially available concrete densifier — invented by Tim Van der Veen after research into how water glass hardens sand dunes in the Netherlands. It targets the free lime inside concrete, converting it into calcium silicate hydrate. Every densifier on the market today exists because Tim proved the concept worked. The original is still the benchmark.

## Source Quote

> We had to make something to harden the free lime. Water glass gave us the direction for what became Hyper Floor — the first densifier.
