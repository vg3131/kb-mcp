# PoliCrete (Marcus): Client Management is the Product

## Quick Answer

Marcos at PoliCrete in Melbourne runs one of the most refined client management systems in the industry. His view: delivering quality concrete is the baseline, but what separates businesses is how you manage the client around the variables you can't control. One misread client or unflagged issue means redoing a day — or the entire job. Client management skills are trainable, but require deliberate development separate from technical skills.

## Source Quote

> We're doing better now because the team is better at managing clients than actual production levels — because one misread client and you're doing the day again.
