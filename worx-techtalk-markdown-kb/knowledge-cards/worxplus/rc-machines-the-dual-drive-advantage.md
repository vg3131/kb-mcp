# RC Machines: The Dual-Drive Advantage

## Quick Answer

All Worx+ RC machines use the dual-drive system invented by Tim Van der Veen — two heads rotating in the same direction with counter-rotation between them. This produces a more even floor profile and eliminates the directional bias found in planetary machines. The system is patented. Tim filed for the patent after HTC sold distribution rights to a competitor — the dual-drive was designed from scratch in two weeks.

## Source Quote

> Gloves are off. I've got a better idea. Tim invented dual drive, filed the patent, and that became everything the RC range is built on.
