# Samples and Pre-Job Testing: Non-Negotiable

## Quick Answer

Works Plus strongly recommends doing a physical sample on every new project — on the actual slab, before quoting the final finish. A sample test reveals how the concrete responds to your process, what gloss level is achievable, and whether there are surface anomalies that change the scope. Contractors who skip samples and go straight to quoting based on photos are the ones who call Works Plus in crisis.

## Source Quote

> Do samples on the actual slab. We really firmly believe in that. We've all had the lesson of not doing one and being burned by it.
