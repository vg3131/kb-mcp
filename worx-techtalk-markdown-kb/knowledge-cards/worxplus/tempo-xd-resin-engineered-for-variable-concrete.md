# Tempo XD Resin: Engineered for Variable Concrete

## Quick Answer

The Tempo XD was developed specifically to reduce the decision-making burden on site when concrete hardness varies. Its matrix sits between soft and hard bonds, performing adequately across a broader range of concrete types. The result: fewer tool changes, fewer calls during projects, and more consistent scratch removal. For contractors who work across mixed residential and commercial environments, it's the most versatile resin in the range.

## Source Quote

> Not all resins are created equal — even within our range. The Tempo XD was developed to sit in the middle of the bond spectrum and handle what most floors throw at you.
