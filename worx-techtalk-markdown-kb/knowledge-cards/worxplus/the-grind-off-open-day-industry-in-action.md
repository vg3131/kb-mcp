# The Grind-Off / Open Day: Industry in Action

## Quick Answer

Works Plus has hosted open day Grind-Off events where contractors attend a free day of machine comparisons, tooling tests, and live demonstrations on prepared slabs. The events are no-cost, include a barbie and sizzle, and are driven by contractor-generated polls about what they want to see. These events let operators test combinations they'd never risk on a client's floor — and generate a full debrief on Tech Talk the following week.
