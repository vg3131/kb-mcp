# The Works Plus Seminar Facility: Behind the Scenes

## Quick Answer

Works Plus runs seminars at its Melbourne headquarters — a dedicated facility with manufacturing, testing, and demonstration areas. Attendees get to see where machines and tooling are made, watch live testing on actual concrete, and tour the behind-the-scenes operations that most contractors never see. The manufacturing area is often the biggest surprise — people know products are made in-house but don't realise the scale.

## Source Quote

> I always get a kick out of showing people around HQ. They get to see the testing and the manufacturing — they geek out a little bit on what's not only in the showroom but what's happening back of house.
