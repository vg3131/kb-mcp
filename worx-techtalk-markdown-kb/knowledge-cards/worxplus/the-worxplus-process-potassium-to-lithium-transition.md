# The Worx+ Process: Potassium to Lithium Transition

## Quick Answer

A critical but often missed step in the process is knowing when to switch densifier types. Potassium silicate is used from grouting stage through to 200 grit resins — it works because the floor still has open pores it can penetrate. After 200 grit, the floor is too closed for potassium. Switch to Totally Boost Lithium at this point — its smaller molecule reaches the remaining micro-pores. Applying potassium after 200 grit is wasted product.

## Source Quote

> Apply a lick coat of Lithium using a microfibre at the 200 grit stage. The floor is too closed for potassium now — lithium finds the pores potassium can no longer reach.
