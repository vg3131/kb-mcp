# The Worx+ Process: Wet vs Dry Resin Stages

## Quick Answer

Each resin stage has a specific moisture requirement. #50 Specialty: fine mist of Totally Boost Potassium 5–10m ahead, run wet. #100 Specialty: even finer mist, run wet. #200 Specialty: run completely dry — no water at all. #400+ Universal/Flex: all dry. This progressively drier sequence matches the increasing density of the floor and prevents glazing, heat buildup, and tool damage.

## Source Quote

> Run the 200 specialty completely dry. No misting. The floor is dense enough that water would hinder rather than help.
