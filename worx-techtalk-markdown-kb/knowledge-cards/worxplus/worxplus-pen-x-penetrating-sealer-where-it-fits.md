# Worx+ Pen-X Penetrating Sealer: Where It Fits

## Quick Answer

The Pen-X is a penetrating reactive sealer — a different category to both Totally Defend (impregnating) and topical coatings. It penetrates the surface and reacts within the concrete structure. It is not a replacement for Totally Defend — each sealer has a specific role. The Pen-X was developed to address questions that neither impregnating nor topical sealers were answering, particularly on denser, more closed surfaces.

## Source Quote

> Where does the Pen-X hit? It fills the gap between what an impregnating sealer does and what a topical does. It's a third option, not a replacement.
